# The Five Sacrifice Prisoners — Full NPC Profiles

**Campaign Context:** Temple of Tiamat finale, Plaza Sacrifice Scene (SCENE-4B-PLAZA-SACRIFICE)

These five prisoners are bound for ritual execution but can be rescued/freed by the party. Each has full stats, personality, and mechanical contribution if saved.

---

## 1. STIRLING — Dwarf Guard Captain

**Role:** Tactical leader. If rescued, can command freed prisoners and provide military intelligence.

### Statistics

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| **Race/Class** | Dwarf Fighter (Champion) |
| **Level** | 5 |
| **CR** | 1/4 (weakened from starvation) |
| **AC** | 12 (leather, no shield — equipment confiscated) |
| **HP** | 24 (3d10 + 6, reduced) |
| **Speed** | 25 ft. |

### Ability Scores

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 14 (+2) |

**Proficiency Bonus:** +3

### Skills & Saves

- **Skills:** Athletics +6, Insight +4, Perception +4
- **Saves:** STR +6, CON +2

### Resistances & Immunities

- Poison resistance (dwarf trait)
- Advantage on saves vs. poison

### Senses

- Darkvision 60 ft.
- Passive Perception 13

### Languages

Common, Dwarvish

---

### Special Traits

**Military Leadership**
Stirling spent 20 years as captain of Baldur's Gate's militia before the caravan raid. If freed and conscious, he can use his action to grant one ally within 30 ft. advantage on their next attack roll or saving throw this turn.

**Battle Scars (Tactical Vigilance)**
Scarred from torture, Stirling has learned to spot danger early. When rolling initiative, if any conscious ally is within 30 feet and Stirling rolled lower than their initiative, Stirling can choose to use his ally's initiative result instead (moving to act at the same time). This represents his protective instinct kicking in. He can use this once per combat.

**Desperate Resolve**
Through sheer willpower, Stirling gains resistance to necrotic damage and advantage on saving throws against being frightened. When he drops to 0 HP, he can immediately make a death save (before other creatures take turns). If successful, he stays conscious at 1 HP but is unconscious on the next failed death save.

---

### Actions

**Greataxe** (if weapon acquired)
- *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target
- *Hit:* 10 (1d12 + 3) slashing damage

**Second Wind** (Fighter feature, 3/day)
Stirling can use a bonus action to regain 1d10 + 2 HP.

---

### Personality & Background

**Before Capture**
Stirling was a respected officer in Baldur's Gate's city watch. He took the caravan commission as a chance to mentor younger guards. The raid that took him was brutal — half his escort was killed outright; he was taken alive because the cultists wanted a "strong subject" for the ritual.

**In Captivity (2 months)**
The starvation and isolation have worn him down physically, but his mind remains sharp. He's spent every day studying the guards' patterns, mapping exits, and planning escape scenarios that never came to fruition. He mentally credits himself with three possible breakouts that failed due to bad luck or insufficient resources.

**When Freed**
Stirling's first emotion is **gratitude**, followed quickly by **tactical assessment**. He will immediately take command instincts kick in — he'll ask:
- "How many guards between here and the exit?"
- "Can we free the others?"
- "What's our defensive position?"

He will not hesitate to fight or die protecting the party if it means getting others to safety.

**Speech & Manner**
Formal, military. He addresses everyone as "soldier" or by rank if known. He uses clipped sentences and speaks directly. Humor is dark and rare, but present. Example: *"Captain Stirling, Baldur's Gate militia — well, was. Now I'm just an angry dwarf with a credit to settle."*

---

### Rescue Mechanics

**If Party Intervenes Before Death:**
- Stirling is slumped forward, bound by magical chains (DC 14 Strength check to break, or Dispel Magic to suppress for 1 minute)
- When freed, he regains consciousness within 1d4 rounds
- He has disadvantage on all attack rolls and ability checks for 3 rounds while he gets his bearings
- After 3 rounds: he fights at full capability

**Emotional Hook**
If the party mentions Baldur's Gate, Waterdeep contacts, or military service, Stirling gains advantage on Persuasion checks to help the party (he respects martial discipline).

**Combat Contribution**
If armed with a weapon:
- Uses his full action economy (action, bonus action, reaction)
- Can cast no spells but can use Second Wind as a bonus action
- Provides flanking for ranged allies
- Can use the Dodge action to protect weaker freed prisoners

### Special Traits

**Merchant's Daughter**
Kess grew up in her family's trading company. She has advantage on Intelligence checks to recall information about:
- Trade routes and merchant networks
- Commodity prices and valuable goods
- City layouts and commercial districts (Waterdeep, Baldur's Gate, etc.)

**Keen Mind (Rogue feature)**
Kess can use a bonus action to study a creature within 30 feet. For the next minute, she has advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks against that creature and advantage on attack rolls against it if it's surprised.

**Cunning Action**
On each of her turns, Kess can use a bonus action to take the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.

**Sneak Attack** (1d6 when conditions met)
Kess can deal bonus damage when she hits with a weapon attack and has advantage, or when an ally is within 5 ft. of the target (Mastermind variant).

**Expertise** (Deception, Sleight of Hand)
Kess doubles her proficiency bonus on Deception and Sleight of Hand checks.

---

### Actions

**Dagger** (if acquired)
- *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target
- *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage

**Insight** (cunning action alternative)
Kess can use a bonus action to read a situation or person, gaining insight into motive or emotion (advantage on next related check).

---

### Personality & Background

**Before Capture**
Kess was a junior merchant in her family's Waterdeep trading company. She was well-connected, travelled regularly, and had a talent for negotiation and reading people. She was on a caravan to Neverwinter when cultists ambushed the guard escort.

**In Captivity (2 months)**
The first month was panic. By month two, Kess had observed the guard rotations, learned the names and routines of the cultists, and mentally catalogued everything that might be useful. She talked to other prisoners, heard their stories, and tried to keep spirits up with dark humor. She's terrified but refuses to show it openly — a mask of resilience hiding deep trauma.

**When Freed**
Kess's first reaction is **survival instinct** — she'll grab a weapon or tool immediately. Her second is **information gathering**: *"What's the situation? How do we get out? Are there other prisoners?"*

She will be shaky at first (disadvantage on first attack roll due to shock) but will stabilize quickly. She becomes a source of calm information and practical thinking.

**Speech & Manner**
Rapid, analytical. She speaks in short declarative sentences when stressed. In calmer moments, she's warm and intelligent. She's quick to compliment and build alliance. Example: *"Okay, okay — we're out. Good move with the distraction. I saw the east passage during meals; it's less guarded. We can make it."*

---

### Rescue Mechanics

**If Party Intervenes Before Death:**
- Kess is conscious but trembling, bound by regular ropes (DC 10 Strength check to break, or she can use a reaction to slip out with Sleight of Hand DC 13)
- When freed, she has disadvantage on attack rolls for 2 rounds due to shock
- She can use Cunning Action from round 1 (Disengage or Hide)
- She will NOT abandon other prisoners mentally — she'll pressure the party to save others

**Emotional Hook**
If the party mentions Waterdeep, trading, or family, Kess opens up about her family and provides backstory (and advantage on Persuasion checks to convince her to stay with the party after escape).

**Combat Contribution**
- Uses Cunning Action to avoid attacks (bonus action Disengage)
- Can provide Sneak Attack if positioned well
- Her real value: **information and morale**. She can identify routes, guard patterns, and vulnerable positions
- Can use Deception to convince other freed prisoners to cooperate
- Can pick simple locks (Thieves' Tools if available)

**Role:** Strategic asset, tactical intelligence, combat partner. If rescued, can provide scouting and ranged support.

### Statistics

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| **Race/Class** | High Elf Ranger (Gloom Stalker) |
| **Level** | 4 |
| **CR** | 1/2 (weakened but dangerous) |
| **AC** | 13 (leather, no bow) |

### Ability Scores

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) |

**Proficiency Bonus:** +2

### Skills & Saves

- **Skills:** Perception +4, Stealth +5, Survival +4
- **Saves:** STR +1, DEX +3

### Resistances & Immunity

- Darkvision 60 ft. (elf trait) + additional 60 ft. (Gloom Stalker)
- Can see in dim light within 120 ft. as if it were bright light
- Can see in darkness within 120 ft. as if it were dim light

### Senses

- Passive Perception 14

### Languages

Common, Elvish, Thieves' Cant (Harper training)

---

### Special Traits

**Harper Agent**
Thorne is a scout and information gatherer for the Harpers. He can use a bonus action to communicate silently with any humanoid within 30 feet who can see him and knows Thieves' Cant. This silent speech can convey simple tactical information or warnings.

**Dread Ambusher** (Gloom Stalker feature)
Thorne has advantage on attack rolls during its first turn of combat. If Thorne is in dim light or darkness when it makes an attack roll, attack roll is made with advantage. Once per turn, Thorne's weapon attacks that deal damage deal an extra 1d8 damage (fire/poison if magic available, otherwise generic).

**Evasion**
If Thorne is subjected to an effect that allows a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, Thorne instead takes no damage if successful, half damage if failed.

**Spellcasting**
Thorne is a 4th-level spellcaster with a spellcasting ability of Wisdom (spell save DC 12, +4 to hit with spell attacks).

**Spells Prepared (limited due to imprisonment):**
- *Cantrips:* Vicious Mockery, Produce Flame
- *1st-level (2 slots):* Goodberry (if he can gather materials), Hunter's Mark
- *2nd-level (1 slot):* Pass Without Trace

---

### Actions

**Shortsword** (if acquired)
- *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target
- *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage, plus 1d8 (Dread Ambusher if in dim light/darkness)

**Ranged (Hand Crossbow or Improvised Missile)**
If he can fashion a weapon or acquire a crossbow:
- *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target
- *Hit:* 6 (1d4 + 3) + 1d8 damage (Dread Ambusher)

**Vicious Mockery** (Cantrip)
Thorne can insult an enemy within 60 feet. Target makes a WIS save DC 12 or takes 1d4 psychic damage and has disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of its next turn.

---

### Personality & Background

**Before Capture**
Thorne was a Harper scout tasked with intelligence gathering on the Cult of the Dragon. He was investigating the Well of Dragons when he was captured by dragonwing patrols. He's been imprisoned for nearly 3 months.

**In Captivity (3 months)**
Thorne has used every moment to study the cultists, map the caverns mentally, and wait for an opportunity. He's learned the guard rotations, identified weaknesses in the enemy's tactical positions, and mentally planned escape routes. The isolation has sharpened his mind into a blade-edge focus. He speaks rarely, listens constantly, and studies every new prisoner for useful information.

**When Freed**
Thorne's first instinct is **strategic assessment**. He will not panic. Instead, he will immediately provide tactical intelligence:
- *"There are 23 cultists on the plaza. Six carrying heavy weapons. Three mages. The main passage north is lightly guarded. We have maybe 90 seconds before the ritual guards reinforce."*

He is calm, professional, and already thinking about the next move.

**Speech & Manner**
Quiet, measured, strategic. He speaks only when necessary and each word carries weight. He addresses people formally and uses their names as a form of respect. Rarely smiles but has dry, cutting humor. Example: *"I've been studying those guards for three months. I can tell you exactly how we kill them — or we can be smart about it and slip past instead. Your choice, but choose quickly."*

---

### Rescue Mechanics

**If Party Intervenes Before Death:**
- Thorne is conscious and watchful, bound by magical chains (DC 15 Strength check to break, or Dispel Magic DC 12)
- When freed, he has NO disadvantage on rolls — his mental conditioning keeps him sharp
- He can immediately use his bonus action to communicate silently with one ally (Harper signal)
- He will provide one piece of tactical intelligence per round (guard positions, exit routes, enemy weaknesses)

**Emotional Hook**
If the party mentions the Harpers, he will confirm his allegiance and offer to provide intelligence reports to the Harper hierarchy. This creates a long-term contact for the party.

**Combat Contribution**
- Can use ranged attacks (hand crossbow, or improvised missile if needed)
- Can cast Vicious Mockery to weaken enemies
- Can use Hide action (Stealth +5) to reposition and gain Dread Ambusher advantage
- Can use Pass Without Trace to help the party escape undetected
- Can scout ahead and provide intelligence on enemy positions
- His real value: **tactical awareness and strategic contribution**. He can tell the party where to move, which enemies are weak, and how to maximize advantage

### Special Traits

**Relentless Endurance**
When Marta is reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright, she can drop to 1 HP instead. Once she uses this trait, she can't use it again until she finishes a long rest.

**Savage Attacks**
When Marta scores a critical hit with a melee weapon attack, she can roll one of the weapon's damage dice one additional time and add it to the extra damage of the critical hit.

**Rage** (3/day, if not yet used today)
As a bonus action, Marta can enter a state of rage for 1 minute. While raging:
- She has advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws
- When she makes a melee weapon attack using Strength, she gains a +2 bonus to the damage roll
- She has resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage
- If Marta falls to 0 HP while raging, she can repeat the save at the end of each of her turns (DC 10 + damage taken since last turn) to stay conscious

**Divine Fury** (Zealot feature)
When Marta hits a target with a melee weapon attack as part of the Attack action, the target takes an extra 1d6 necrotic damage. This scales with level but is limited by her imprisonment.

**Avenging Angel** (potential trigger)
Marta has a daughter and son (both taken by cultists, fate unknown). If she learns they are alive, she will immediately demand to find them. If she learns they are dead, she enters an uncontrollable rage (automatically Rages on her next turn, cannot end rage until she's killed at least one cultist).

---

### Actions

**Greatclub** (if acquired) or **Unarmed Strike**
- *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target
- *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage, plus 1d6 necrotic (Divine Fury)

**Intimidate**
Marta can use her action to attempt to frighten a target within 30 feet who can see her. Target makes a WIS save DC 10. On a failure, the target is Frightened for 1 minute. If frightened, the target has disadvantage on the next attack roll or saving throw before the end of its next turn.

---

### Personality & Background

**Before Capture**
Marta was a simple farmwoman living outside Waterdeep with her two children (Kael, 8 years old, and Senna, 6). She was captured during a cultist raid that burned the farm. She believes her children were taken by the cultists but doesn't know their fate. This uncertainty is a constant pain.

**In Captivity (3 months)**
Marta has oscillated between desperate hope and crushing despair. She's tried to stay strong for the other prisoners, but the stress has left her raw and dangerous. She's managed to stay out of fights because the moment she starts fighting, she doesn't know if she can stop. The grief has hollowed her out — only rage remains.

**When Freed**
Marta's first reaction is **confusion**, quickly followed by **desperate questioning**:
- *"Where are my children? Are they alive? Where is Kael? Where is Senna?"*

If the party can offer her hope that her children might be alive in the temple, she becomes **unstoppable**. If she learns they're dead, she becomes a force of pure vengeance.

**Speech & Manner**
Simple, direct, intense. She speaks with a farmwoman's practicality but underneath is raw emotion barely held in check. She uses her children's names frequently (a way of maintaining connection). Example: *"My Kael and Senna — they're good kids, brave kids. I need to find them. Whatever it takes."*

---

### Rescue Mechanics

**If Party Intervenes Before Death:**
- Marta is conscious but grief-stricken, bound by regular ropes (DC 11 Strength check to break, or she can tear them free with a Rage action)
- When freed, she will demand information about her children immediately
- She has disadvantage on attack rolls for 1 round due to emotional shock
- After 1 round, she can access her Rage feature

**Emotional Hook (CRITICAL):**
This is THE critical emotional anchor for Marta. If the party can:
- Offer to find her children
- Promise to reunite them
- Or (tragically) confirm her children's fates

...then Marta becomes emotionally bound to the party. She will fight to the death for them. She will rage for them. She is no longer a freed prisoner — she is an avenging ally with a cause.

**Combat Contribution**
- Can Rage for +2 damage and advantage on Strength checks
- Can use Relentless Endurance to stay in combat when downed
- Can use Intimidate action to frighten enemies
- Her real value: **raw strength and emotional investment**. When she fights, she fights WITH the party, not just alongside them. Her vengeance becomes their cause.

### Special Traits

**Sorcerous Origin — Wild Magic (Corrupted)**
Fen has innate magical ability, but it has been twisted by cult rituals. His spellcasting is chaotic and unreliable.

**Tides of Chaos** (Feature, 1/day if not used)
Fen can manipulate probability around him. He can gain advantage on one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw within the next minute.

**Wild Magic Surge** (Triggered when casting a spell)
When Fen casts a leveled spell, there's a 20% chance (roll 1d20; on 1-4) that a wild magic surge occurs. Roll on a simplified surge table: the spell might backfire (heals enemy instead of harming), duplicates unexpectedly, affects wrong target, etc.

**Mind Programming** (Cult Ritual Effect)
Fen has been conditioned by cult rituals to:
- Respond to trigger words/phrases with automatic actions (singing ritual words, freezing in place, attacking allies)
- Feel compelled to complete the sacrifice ritual
- Believe he is "ascending" through the ritual (redemption through death)

**Dormant Sorcerous Potential**
If deprogrammed and mentored, Fen could grow into a powerful sorcerer. He has 4 additional spell slots that are currently "locked" by cult conditioning.

---

### Actions

**Mage Hand** (Cantrip)
Fen can create a spectral hand within 30 feet to interact with objects (lift, move, manipulate).

**Fire Bolt** (Cantrip, if cult-taught)
Fen can cast a bolt of fire at a target within 120 feet. Target makes a DEX save DC 12. On a failure, it takes 1d10 fire damage.

**Burning Hands** (1st-level spell, cult-conditioned)
Fen can cast a wave of burning hands in a 15-foot cone. Creatures in the area make a DEX save DC 12, taking 3d6 fire damage on a failure, half on a success.

**Cure Wounds** (1st-level spell, if taught by allies)
Fen can touch a creature and restore 1d8 + 2 HP. This requires concentration and only works if Fen is in a state of clarity (not cult-programmed).

---

### Personality & Background

**Before Cult Involvement**
Fen was a street orphan in Neverwinter. He had latent magical ability but no way to develop it. He was alone, hungry, and desperate. The Cult of the Dragon found him and offered him:
- Food, shelter, belonging
- Purpose and identity
- The promise of "ascension" through the ritual

He joined willingly at first — he thought he was finally part of something.

**Under Cult Control (4 months)**
The cult performed rituals to "awaken" Fen's magic and bind his will. He was taught spells, conditioned with trigger words, and trained to believe the sacrifice ritual would elevate him to a higher plane. He has spent the last month singing ritual words, believing he's preparing for his own transcendence. The irony: he doesn't realize HE is the sacrifice.

**When Freed**
Fen's immediate reaction is **confusion**. He's been conditioned to complete the ritual. When the party intervenes, he might:
- Continue singing the ritual words (automatic response to trigger)
- Freeze in confusion (cognitive dissonance between programming and rescue)
- Lash out with magic (scared, defensive)
- Break down in tears when he realizes what the ritual actually is (death, not ascension)

He will need time to deprogram and trust the party.

**Speech & Manner**
Rapid, almost childlike. He speaks in short phrases, sometimes with ritual words mixed in. Before cult conditioning, he was probably warm and curious. Now he's fragile, traumatized, and dangerous in his unpredictability. Example (in cult state): *"The Queen calls... I will ascend... the ritual completes..."* vs. (after rescue): *"I— I don't understand. What's happening? Am I... am I dying?"*

---

### Rescue Mechanics

**If Party Intervenes Before Death:**
- Fen is conscious but chanting ritual words in a trance state
- He is NOT magically bound but is psychologically compelled to complete the ritual
- To snap him out of the trance: DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check or 1 successful spell that breaks mind control (Dispel Magic, Lesser Restoration if possible)
- When freed, he is confused and disoriented for 1 round (cannot take actions, can only move or use reaction)
- He will NOT immediately fight for the party — he will be confused and frightened

**Emotional Hook (CRITICAL):**
Fen needs a mentor. If one party member (Axar the Wizard is a good thematic fit, or any caster) takes him under their wing and promises to:
- Teach him real magic (not cult perversion)
- Help him understand his power
- Offer belonging without compulsion

...then Fen becomes emotionally attached to that character. He will fight alongside the party, particularly to protect his new mentor.

**Deprogramming Arc:**
Over the course of the campaign (or in a post-campaign epilogue), Fen can:
- Regain access to his locked spell slots
- Learn to control his Wild Magic Surges
- Become a proper ally caster
- Potentially become a full party member or NPC ally for future campaigns

**Combat Contribution** (Early)
- Very limited — mostly supports with cantrips and 1st-level spells
- His real value: **emotional core and redemption potential**. He represents what the party is fighting against (mind control, forced sacrifice) and what they're fighting for (freedom, choice, hope).
- As campaign progresses: can unlock more spellcasting ability if trained

## DM Notes

**These prisoners are NOT meant to overshadow the party.** They are:
- Weaker than level 15 PCs
- Emotionally vulnerable, not fully combat-capable
- Story anchors, not reinforcements
- Opportunities for moral choice, not easy wins

**Use them to:**
- Increase emotional stakes of the sacrifice scene
- Reward the party for creative problem-solving (saving prisoners)
- Create long-term relationship hooks
- Demonstrate the human cost of the ritual

**Balance considerations:**
- If all 5 are freed AND join combat, the party has 5 additional allies
- This is powerful but thematic (the party is rallying others to fight Tiamat)
- Recommend limiting to 2-3 active freed prisoners in any one combat to avoid overwhelming the action economy
- Others can fight, but use simplified actions (Attack only, no bonus actions) to speed play
