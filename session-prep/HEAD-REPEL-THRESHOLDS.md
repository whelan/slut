## 💥 Head Repel Thresholds

The heads cannot be **killed** — they can be **repelled** back through the portal. When a head reaches its threshold, it withdraws screaming and is out for the rest of the ritual.

| Head | Threshold | Damage taken (R7) | Remaining | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **White** (frost) | **150** | 110 | **40** | One good hit from repelled |
| **Black** (acid) | **160** | 5 | 155 | Barely touched |
| **Green** (poison) | **170** | 0 | 170 | Active |
| **Blue** (lightning) | **180** | 0 | 180 | Emerged Round 7 |
| **Red** (fire) | **200** | — | — | Emerges Round 8 |

- **AC 22** on all heads (unchanged)
- **Bite:** +14 to hit, 4d6+8 piercing + 4d8 elemental (matching color), targets lowest-HP PC in 30 ft reach
- Existing repel tricks still work (Athletics DC 18 Frygtløs / 22 others = pushed back 1 round; 50+ radiant = blinded 1 round; Persuasion/Performance DC 20 = distracted 1 round)

**Reward for repelling a head:** That head is *dazed* when Tiamat manifests — in Rounds 9-10 she loses that head's attack AND breath weapon for the **first 2 rounds** of the finale fight.
