# Temple of Tiamat – 10-Round Ritual Breakdown

**The ritual runs 10 combat rounds.** Rounds 1–3 are the "disruption window." Heads appear at rounds 4–8. Tiamat fully manifests at round 8. Rounds 9–10 show her in full combat.

---

## ⚪ ROUND 1: The Ritual Begins

**Ritual Clock Advance:** DC 12 check at end of round

**Tiamat Status:** No manifestation yet. Portal pulses with eldritch light.

---

### **Rath Modar** (Red Chapel)
- **Action:** Begins ritual coordination. Moves between Red Chapel viewing area; maintains concentration on **Globe of Invulnerability** (already cast, pre-combat).
- **Bonus Action:** Uses ritual focus to confirm all five Red Wizards are in position and concentrating.
- **Reaction:** Ready to defend the ritual if party breaches the chapel.
- **Spell Protection:** Globe surrounds the chapel ritual zone (15-foot radius). No spell of 4th level or lower can enter.
- **Demeanor:** Calm, focused. Confident the ritual will complete.

### **Severin** (Sanctuary, at portal)
- **Action:** Begins chanting the ritual incantation. Stands motionless, arms raised toward the portal.
- **Bonus Action – Ritual Action:** Clock +1. The ritual officially advances to Round 1.
- **Reaction:** None (will hold for combat interruptions).
- **Status:** Concentrating on the ritual channel (not on a spell yet).
- **Appearance:** The Mask of the Dragon Queen gleams on his face; eldritch energy flows from the portal through his spine.

### **Red Wizards at Chapels (4 grounded)**
**Magus Azuri** (Blue Chapel – Entrance)
- **Action:** Moves to the chapel's focus point; begins ritual chanting with other wizards.
- **Bonus Action:** Casts **Mage Armor** (concentration, AC 16).
- **Status:** Focused on ritual. Will not engage unless forced.

**Magus Alabaster** (White Chapel)
- **Action:** Takes position at focus, begins chanting.
- **Bonus Action:** Casts **Mirror Image** (concentration, 3 images).
- **Preparation:** Ready to cast Counterspell if interrupted.

**Magus Verdian** (Green Chapel)
- **Action:** Ritual focus; begins chanting in unison with others.
- **Bonus Action:** Casts **Blur** (concentration, AC harder to hit).
- **Stance:** Alert but committed to ritual.

**Magus Obsidian** (Black Chapel – Exit)
- **Action:** Moves to exit-guard position; begins chanting.
- **Bonus Action:** Casts **Mage Armor** (concentration, AC 16).
- **Duty:** Guards the ritual zone. First to engage if party enters from below.

### **Red Wizards at Spires (5 flying)**
**Magus Cerulean** (Blue Spire)
- **Action:** Takes aerial position 40 feet above the main ritual chamber; begins ritual chanting.
- **Bonus Action:** Casts **Levitate** (concentration) to stay aloft.
- **Stance:** Eyes scanning for intruders from the upper levels.

**Magus Platinum** (White Spire)
- **Action:** Ritual focus (aerial); begins chanting.
- **Bonus Action:** Casts **Mage Armor** (AC 16).
- **Posture:** Hovering, alert.

**Magus Viridian** (Green Spire)
- **Action:** Ritual focus; begins chanting.
- **Bonus Action:** Casts **Blur** (concentration).
- **Vigilance:** Scanning for intrusion points.

**Magus Obsidian II** (Black Spire)
- **Action:** Ritual focus (aerial); begins chanting.
- **Bonus Action:** Casts **Mage Armor** (AC 16).
- **Role:** Overwatch from above.

**Magus Vermillion** (Red Spire)
- **Action:** Ritual focus; begins chanting.
- **Bonus Action:** Casts **Blur** (concentration).
- **Aggression:** Most eager to cast offensive spells if interrupted.

---

**End of Round 1:** 
- Ritual Check (DC 12): If failed, ritual holds at Round 1. If disrupted (wizard killed, etc.), DC rises by +1.
- **Tiamat:** Still dormant. Portal flickers faintly with white light.

---

## ⚪ ROUND 2: Momentum Builds

**Ritual Clock Advance:** DC 12 check at end of round (or higher if disrupted)

**Tiamat Status:** Portal brightens. A faint shadow moves behind the gate.

---

### **Rath Modar** (Red Chapel)
- **Action:** Maintains concentration on **Globe of Invulnerability**; watches for ritual disruptions.
- **Bonus Action:** Ritual coordination. If any Red Wizard is engaged in combat, Rath Modar is aware.
- **Reaction:** Ready to cast **Counterspell** if a party member attempts to disrupt the ritual zone.
- **Movement:** Moves 20 ft. closer to the portal if the party has not engaged him yet (repositioning for better view).

### **Severin** (Sanctuary)
- **Action:** Continues chanting; ritual energy grows visibly around the portal.
- **Bonus Action – Ritual Action:** Clock +1 (advances ritual to Round 2).
- **Reaction:** None yet.
- **Manifestation:** The portal behind Severin pulses with increasing frequency. The light shifts from white to deeper colors.

### **Red Wizards at Chapels (4 grounded)**
- **Action:** Continue ritual chanting. If no party members have engaged them, they remain focused and immobile.
- **Bonus Action:** Each wizard readies a reaction spell (Counterspell or Shield) in case of attack.
- **Status:** All 4 still at their focus points, concentrating on the ritual.

**Magus Azuri** – Ready to cast **Counterspell**.
**Magus Alabaster** – Ready to cast **Shield**.
**Magus Verdian** – Ready to cast **Counterspell**.
**Magus Obsidian** – Ready to cast **Acid Splash** or move to intercept.

### **Red Wizards at Spires (5 flying)**
- **Action:** Aerial vigil; ritual chanting continues.
- **Bonus Action:** Each wizard readies a ranged attack or spell (Firebolt, Frostbolt, etc.) if party members approach from below.
- **Status:** All 5 maintain altitude (20–50 feet above the chamber).

**Magus Cerulean** – Ready to cast **Firebolt**.
**Magus Platinum** – Ready to cast **Frostbolt**.
**Magus Viridian** – Ready to cast **Poison Spray**.
**Magus Obsidian II** – Ready to cast **Acid Splash**.
**Magus Vermillion** – Ready to cast **Firebolt**.

---

**End of Round 2:**
- **Ritual Check:** DC 12 (or higher if Red Wizards have been killed).
- **Tiamat:** Portal dims and brightens in a steady pulse. The shadow behind the gate becomes more defined (a draconic silhouette).

---

## 🔥 ROUND 3: Last Chance to Disrupt

**Ritual Clock Advance:** DC 12 check at end of round

**Tiamat Status:** Portal blazes with eldritch fire. A low rumble echoes through the temple. You hear breathing from the other side.

**DM Note:** This is the LAST ROUND before heads begin to emerge. If the party does not kill at least one Red Wizard or damage Severin's concentration, Tiamat's manifestation becomes inevitable.

---

### **Rath Modar** (Red Chapel)
- **Action:** Ritual coordination intensifies. Rath Modar's voice rises as he chants in Infernal alongside Severin.
- **Bonus Action – Ritual Action (if Rath had one):** Actually, Rath Modar does NOT have his own Ritual Action. That is Severin's role. However, Rath Modar can use his action to cast a **Fireball** (save DC 17) if party members approach within 60 feet.
- **Alternative Action:** If party is close, Rath Modar uses **Counterspell** to shut down party spell attempts.
- **Movement:** Moves 30 feet toward the center of the chapel, positioning himself between party and the ritual focus.
- **Magical Aura:** The **Globe of Invulnerability** expands slightly (still 15-foot radius but visibly more powerful).

### **Severin** (Sanctuary)
- **Action:** The ritual chant reaches a crescendo. Severin's voice echoes unnaturally through the temple—it is no longer just his voice, but layered with others. The Portal writhes.
- **Bonus Action – Ritual Action:** Clock +1 (advances to Round 3, the final pre-manifestation round).
- **Reaction:** If a party member attempts to destroy a mask or interrupt the ritual directly, Severin will use **Shield** reaction or cast **Counterspell** (prepared).
- **Appearance:** Severin's eyes glow with eldritch light. The Mask of the Dragon Queen is almost unrecognizable—it has merged with his face, becoming one.

### **Red Wizards at Chapels (4 grounded)**
- **Action:** Ritual chanting reaches a fever pitch. All four wizards are locked in deep concentration, their hands glowing with ritual energy.
- **Bonus Action:** Each wizard casts a **Counterspell-ready** reaction. If a party member casts a spell to disrupt the ritual, that wizard can use their reaction to cancel it.
- **Status:** Immobile, fully committed to the ritual. If attacked, they will defend themselves (action to cast a spell or counterspell), but breaking concentration will interrupt the ritual.

**All four wizards:** Concentrating on **Shield**, ready to dodge incoming attacks or cast **Counterspell**.

### **Red Wizards at Spires (5 flying)**
- **Action:** Aerial chanting reaches crescendo. The five flying wizards form a defensive pattern, their combined magical auras interlocking in mid-air.
- **Bonus Action:** Each wizard is ready to cast an offensive spell (Fireball, Lighting Bolt, Cone of Cold, etc.) if the party moves to interrupt.
- **Status:** All 5 wizards maintain altitude and continue ritual chanting.

**Magus Cerulean** – Ready to cast **Lightning Bolt** (3d8, DC 16 DEX save).
**Magus Platinum** – Ready to cast **Cone of Cold** (6d8, DC 16 CON save).
**Magus Viridian** – Ready to cast **Stinking Cloud** (concentration, blocks vision).
**Magus Obsidian II** – Ready to cast **Acid Splash** or **Melf's Acid Arrow** (2d4 acid).
**Magus Vermillion** – Ready to cast **Fireball** (save DC 17, 8d6 fire).

---

**End of Round 3:**
- **Ritual Check:** DC 12 (or +1 for each Red Wizard killed so far).
  - **Success:** The ritual advances to Round 4. Tiamat's first head emerges.
  - **Failure (rare):** The ritual is held at Round 3 for one more round. The party has another chance to disrupt.
- **Tiamat:** The portal tears open. The white, frosted shape of a draconic head pushes through the barrier—scales of pure ice, breath that frosts the air, ancient eyes of glacial blue.

---

## ❄️ ROUND 4: WHITE HEAD EMERGES

**Ritual Clock:** 4 / Heads through portal: 1 (White)

**Tiamat Status:** White dragon head fully emerged through portal (50% of head visible). Breathing heavily. Preparing to attack.

**Environmental Effect:** 15-foot radius zone centered on the portal. All creatures in the zone at the end of their turn take **2d6 cold damage** (DC 13 CON save for half). The temperature drops to arctic levels. Ice forms on all surfaces.

---

### **Tiamat's White Head** (New combatant!)
- **Action:** Takes a moment to adjust to the Material Plane; no action yet.
- **Bonus Action:** None.
- **Reaction:** Will be ready next round.
- **End of Round:** At the end of Round 4, the White head makes a **Bite attack** (+14 to hit) against the lowest-HP target visible. Damage: **3d6+6 piercing + 1d6 cold**.
- **At Recharge (5–6):** The White head uses **Cone of Cold** breath weapon (30-foot cone, 7d6 cold, DC 15 CON save for half).

### **Rath Modar** (Red Chapel)
- **Action:** Sees the White head emerge. Maintains concentration on **Globe of Invulnerability** but now shifts to offensive posture.
- **Bonus Action:** If Rath Modar has not been engaged, he casts **Fireball** (save DC 17, 8d6 fire) toward the party's position, attempting to flush them out of defensive positions.
- **Movement:** Moves 30 feet closer to the portal to have a better vantage on the emerging Tiamat.
- **Demeanor:** Triumphant. "The Queen rises! You cannot stop this!"

### **Severin** (Sanctuary)
- **Action:** Continues channeling ritual energy to stabilize Tiamat's emergence. Does not attack yet.
- **Bonus Action – Ritual Action:** Clock +1 (advances to Round 4). "The White head emerges! The ritual is unstoppable!"
- **Reaction:** Ready to use **Shield** or **Counterspell** if interrupted.
- **Concentration:** Still locked in on the ritual channel.

### **Red Wizards at Chapels (4 grounded)**
- **Action:** All four wizards shift from ritual focus to defensive combat stance.
- **Bonus Action:** Each wizard casts an offensive spell:
  - **Magus Azuri:** Casts **Lightning Bolt** (4d6 lightning, DC 16 DEX save) toward the nearest party member.
  - **Magus Alabaster:** Casts **Cone of Cold** (6d8 cold, DC 16 CON save).
  - **Magus Verdian:** Casts **Stinking Cloud** (blocks vision, blocks spells).
  - **Magus Obsidian:** Casts **Acid Splash** or **Scorching Burst** depending on proximity.
- **Status:** All four are now in active combat support. They will continue casting offensive spells each round.

### **Red Wizards at Spires (5 flying)**
- **Action:** All five flying wizards shift to aggressive aerial combat.
- **Bonus Action:** Each wizard casts an offensive spell:
  - **Magus Cerulean:** Casts **Lightning Bolt** (4d6 lightning, DC 16 DEX save).
  - **Magus Platinum:** Casts **Cone of Cold** (6d8 cold, DC 16 CON save).
  - **Magus Viridian:** Casts **Insect Plague** (concentration, 4d10 piercing, fills a 20-ft. radius).
  - **Magus Obsidian II:** Casts **Scorching Burst** (3d6 fire around one target, DC 16 DEX half).
  - **Magus Vermillion:** Casts **Fireball** (8d6 fire, save DC 17, 20-ft. radius).
- **Altitude:** All 5 wizards rise to 50+ feet, using their height advantage to rain spells down on the party.

---

**End of Round 4:**
- **White Head Bite:** Attacks lowest-HP target. Bite +14 to hit, **3d6+6 piercing + 1d6 cold** (19 damage avg. before save).
- **Environment:** Cold damage zone activates. Anyone in the 15-foot zone takes **2d6 cold** (DC 13 CON save).
- **Ritual Check:** DC 13 (was 12, +1 for ongoing ritual progress). Does the ritual continue?

---

## ⚫ ROUND 5: BLACK HEAD EMERGES + SEVERIN ASCENDS

**Ritual Clock:** 5 / Heads through portal: 2 (White, Black)

**Tiamat Status:** Black head now pushes through the portal alongside White. Acid begins to pool on the ground. The temple shudders.

**Environmental Effect:** Acid spreads in a 20-foot radius around the portal. Creatures at the end of their turn take **2d6 acid damage** (DC 13 CON save for half). Both cold and acid hazards are now active simultaneously.

**Severin's Ascension:** At the start of Round 5, Severin gains **Champion of Tiamat** blessing: **+2 to all attack rolls and saving throws** (lasts until ritual ends or Severin dies).

---

### **White & Black Heads** (Active combatants!)
- **Action:** Both heads are now partially through the portal. Each can take a limited action (no movement yet).
- **End of Round Attacks:**
  - **White Head Bite:** +14 to hit, **3d6+6 piercing + 1d6 cold**.
  - **Black Head Bite:** +14 to hit, **3d6+6 piercing + 2d6 acid**.
- **Breath Weapons (Recharge 5–6):**
  - **White:** Cone of Cold (7d6 cold, 30-ft. cone, DC 15 CON save).
  - **Black:** Acid Cloud (similar range, 7d6 acid, DC 15 CON save).

### **Rath Modar** (Red Chapel)
- **Action:** Sees two heads emerging. Shifts to pure offensive support.
- **Action:** Casts **Wall of Fire** (concentration, 60-foot line or 20-ft. radius). Positioned to trap party members between the fire and incoming acid/cold hazards.
- **Bonus Action:** Uses **Misty Step** to reposition 30 feet away from party threats.
- **Movement:** Stays mobile, maintains **Globe of Invulnerability** around the chapel ritual focus.
- **Demeanor:** "The Black head rises! The Queen's fury is unleashed!"

### **Severin** (Sanctuary)
- **Action:** The ritual reaches a critical juncture. Severin's eyes open wide, glowing with pure eldritch power. The Mask of the Dragon Queen blazes with light.
- **Bonus Action – Ritual Action:** Clock +1 (advances to Round 5).
- **NEW:** Severin gains **+2 to all attack rolls and saving throws** (Champion of Tiamat blessing).
- **Legendary Action (if combat has begun):** Uses **Ritual Surge** (2 LA): Clock +1 immediately, all allied cultists on Level 3 heal **2d8**.
- **Concentration:** Now splits between ritual and combat spells. Will cast **Fireball** (save DC 17, 8d6 fire) or **Scorching Burst** if enemies are close.

### **Red Wizards at Chapels (4 grounded)**
- **Action:** All four wizards have sensed the power surge. They press their attacks harder.
- **Action:** Each casts a **2nd-level spell**:
  - **Magus Azuri:** **Scorching Burst** (3d6 fire, DC 16 DEX half).
  - **Magus Alabaster:** **Blindness/Deafness** (DC 16 CON save, blinds one target).
  - **Magus Verdian:** **Phantasmal Force** (illusion, DC 16 INT save, 2d6 psychic per round).
  - **Magus Obsidian:** **Heat Metal** (if a PC is wearing metal armor, DC 16 CON save, 2d8 fire).
- **Bonus Action:** Each wizard casts a **cantrip** (Firebolt, Frostbolt, Acid Splash, etc.).
- **Movement:** Move 20 feet to maintain distance from party melee characters.

### **Red Wizards at Spires (5 flying)**
- **Action:** The five flying wizards sense Severin's ascension. They intensify their spell assault.
- **Action:** Each casts a **higher-level spell**:
  - **Magus Cerulean:** **Lightning Bolt** (5d6 lightning, DC 16 DEX save).
  - **Magus Platinum:** **Cone of Cold** (8d8 cold, DC 16 CON save).
  - **Magus Viridian:** **Cloudkill** (concentration, fills a 20-ft. radius with poisonous gas, 5d8 poison, DC 15 CON save).
  - **Magus Obsidian II:** **Acid Arrow** (ranged attack, 4d4 acid on hit, 2d4 per round continuation).
  - **Magus Vermillion:** **Fireball** (9d6 fire, save DC 17, 20-ft. radius).
- **Movement:** All wizards maintain 50+ feet altitude. Tactics: attack from above, avoid melee range.

---

**End of Round 5:**
- **Acid Damage Zone:** All creatures in 20-ft. radius of portal take **2d6 acid** (DC 13 CON save).
- **Ritual Check:** DC 14 (was 13, +1 for ritual progress). If successful, ritual advances to Round 6.

---

## 🟢 ROUND 6: GREEN HEAD EMERGES + FIRST TRUE BITE

**Ritual Clock:** 6 / Heads through portal: 3 (White, Black, Green)

**Tiamat Status:** Green head pushes through alongside the other two. Poison clouds billow from her maw. The temple itself seems to warp in her presence.

**Environmental Effect:** Poison and illusion auras now fill the chamber. Creatures end their turn in the 25-foot radius around the portal take **2d6 poison damage** (DC 13 CON save for half). Additionally, creatures must make a DC 15 Insight check to identify what is real and what is an illusion (disadvantage if affected).

**Combat Escalation:** The White and Black heads are now fully capable of attacking. They attack at the END of Round 6 for the first time (having charged up from previous rounds).

---

### **White, Black, & Green Heads** (Full attack active!)
- **Initiative:** All three heads attack at the end of Round 6 (and every round thereafter).
- **White Head Bite:** +14 to hit, **3d6+6 piercing + 1d6 cold**. Targets lowest-HP PC.
- **Black Head Bite:** +14 to hit, **3d6+6 piercing + 2d6 acid**. Targets 2nd-lowest-HP PC.
- **Green Head Bite:** +14 to hit, **3d6+6 piercing + 1d6 poison**. Targets 3rd-lowest-HP PC.
- **Breath Weapons (Recharge 5–6):**
  - Each head has a 50% chance to use its breath this round.
  - **White:** Cone of Cold (7d6 cold, DC 15 CON).
  - **Black:** Acid Cloud (7d6 acid, DC 15 CON).
  - **Green:** Poison Breath (7d6 poison, DC 15 CON, + creatures are Poisoned for 1 minute on failed save).

### **Rath Modar** (Red Chapel)
- **Action:** Sees three heads and realizes the ritual is unstoppable. Shifts fully to offense.
- **Action:** Casts **Confusion** (8d6 targets in 20-ft. radius, DC 16 WIS save). Targets party members.
- **Movement:** Uses **Misty Step** to reposition and maintain safe distance (30+ feet from melee).
- **Concentration:** Now concentrating on **Confusion** (drops **Wall of Fire** or **Globe**).
- **Appearance:** Rath Modar's silhouette against the portal shows three dragon heads. He raises his arms in triumph: "THREE HEADS! THE QUEEN RISES IN FULL GLORY!"

### **Severin** (Sanctuary)
- **Action:** Severin's ritual power is absolute now. He no longer needs to concentrate fully on the ritual channel—it sustains itself.
- **Bonus Action – Ritual Action:** Clock +1 (advances to Round 6).
- **Action:** Now Severin casts offensive spells to defend the ritual. Casts **Fireball** (save DC 17, 8d6 fire, 20-ft. radius) or **Hellish Chains** (one creature within 30 feet, restrained, 3d6 fire damage).
- **Legendary Actions (3/round):**
  - **1. Cantrip:** Eldritch Blast (two beams, +9 to hit, 1d10+5 force each).
  - **2. Ritual Surge:** Clock +1 immediately. All allied cultists on Level 3 heal **2d8**. (Use this if Severin has not acted in the round.)
  - **3. Summon Devout:** 1d4 Dragonclaw guards teleport up from Level 1. They act next round.

### **Red Wizards at Chapels (4 grounded)**
- **Action:** The four chapel wizards sense the Green head's emergence. They begin casting coordinated spells.
- **Action:** Each casts a **damaging spell**:
  - **Magus Azuri:** **Fireball** (8d6 fire, save DC 17, toward party cluster).
  - **Magus Alabaster:** **Cone of Cold** (8d8 cold, DC 16 CON save).
  - **Magus Verdian:** **Cloudkill** (20-ft. radius, 5d8 poison, DC 15 CON save).
  - **Magus Obsidian:** **Stinking Cloud** (20-ft. radius, blocks vision and spells).
- **Bonus Action:** Each casts **Firebolt** or **Frostbolt** cantrip as bonus action.
- **Movement:** All four maintain 20+ feet distance from melee threats.

### **Red Wizards at Spires (5 flying)**
- **Action:** The five aerial wizards begin dropping heavy spells from above.
- **Action:** Each casts a **high-level spell**:
  - **Magus Cerulean:** **Lightning Bolt** (6d6 lightning, DC 16 DEX save).
  - **Magus Platinum:** **Cone of Cold** (9d8 cold, DC 16 CON save).
  - **Magus Viridian:** **Insect Plague** (20-ft. radius, 5d10 piercing, concentration).
  - **Magus Obsidian II:** **Scorching Burst** (4d6 fire, DC 16 DEX half damage).
  - **Magus Vermillion:** **Fireball** (9d6 fire, save DC 17, 20-ft. radius).
- **Altitude:** All maintain 60+ feet height to avoid melee retaliation.

---

**End of Round 6:**
- **Three Heads Attack:** White bite, Black bite, Green bite all land (if they hit). Expect **15–25 damage per head** against PCs in the 15-ft. zone.
- **Poison Damage Zone:** Creatures in 25-ft. radius take **2d6 poison** (DC 13 CON save).
- **Ritual Check:** DC 15 (was 14, +1 for ritual progress). If successful, ritual advances to Round 7.

---

## 🔵 ROUND 7: BLUE HEAD EMERGES + SEVERIN IMMORTAL

**Ritual Clock:** 7 / Heads through portal: 4 (White, Black, Green, Blue)

**Tiamat Status:** Blue head crashes through the portal with a deafening roar. Four dragon heads are now visible, breathing elemental fury. The temple cracks.

**Environmental Effect:** Lightning arcs across the chamber (25-foot radius from portal). Creatures at the end of their turn take **2d6 lightning damage** (DC 13 DEX save for half). All previous hazards (cold, acid, poison) remain active.

**Severin's Immortality:** At the start of Round 7, Severin becomes **unkillable**. He cannot drop below 3 HP. (Reflective of Tiamat's protection of her chosen vessel.)

---

### **White, Black, Green, & Blue Heads** (Full assault!)
- **Initiative:** All four heads attack at the end of Round 7.
- **White Head Bite:** +14 to hit, **3d6+6 piercing + 1d6 cold**.
- **Black Head Bite:** +14 to hit, **3d6+6 piercing + 2d6 acid**.
- **Green Head Bite:** +14 to hit, **3d6+6 piercing + 1d6 poison**.
- **Blue Head Bite:** +14 to hit, **3d6+6 piercing + 2d6 lightning**. NEW HEAD – targets 4th-lowest-HP PC.
- **Breath Weapons (Recharge 5–6):**
  - **White/Blue:** Each has 50% chance to use Cone of Cold / Chain Lightning.
  - **Black/Green:** Each has 50% chance to use Acid Cloud / Poison Breath.

### **Rath Modar** (Red Chapel)
- **Action:** Rath Modar watches FOUR heads emerge. He steps back from his position and begins casting **Mislead** (concentration). He becomes invisible and creates an illusory duplicate.
- **Bonus Action:** Uses **Counterspell** (reaction ready) against any party spell that targets the ritual.
- **Movement:** The real Rath Modar moves 30 feet toward the portal's inner sanctum, hidden from view by Mislead's illusion.
- **Demeanor:** "FOUR! THE QUEEN'S WRATH IS UNSTOPPABLE! SEVERIN IS BEYOND YOUR REACH NOW!"

### **Severin** (Sanctuary)
- **Action:** At the start of Round 7, the Mask of the Dragon Queen glows with blinding light. Severin's form seems to shimmer with otherworldly power.
- **NEW ABILITY:** Severin gains **Tiamat's Protection**: He cannot be reduced below 3 HP. Any damage that would reduce him below 3 HP is instead reduced to 3 HP.
- **Bonus Action – Ritual Action:** Clock +1 (advances to Round 7).
- **Action:** Casts **Dominate Monster** (DC 20 WIS save) on the strongest melee character (typically **Frygtløs**). If successful, Frygtløs must make a WIS save or be charmed and under Severin's control.
- **Legendary Action – Ritual Surge (2 LA):** Clock +1 immediately. All allied Red Wizards and cultists heal **2d8 HP**.
- **Demeanor:** "SEVERIN CANNOT FALL! THE QUEEN PROTECTS ME! THE RITUAL IS COMPLETE!"

### **Red Wizards at Chapels (4 grounded)**
- **Action:** All four chapel wizards are in full combat mode. They sense Severin's ascension and press harder.
- **Action:** Each casts a **devastating spell**:
  - **Magus Azuri:** **Cone of Cold** (9d8 cold, DC 16 CON save).
  - **Magus Alabaster:** **Blindness/Deafness** on all party members in range (DC 16 CON save each).
  - **Magus Verdian:** **Stinking Cloud** (creates zones to block vision and spells).
  - **Magus Obsidian:** **Melf's Acid Arrow** (ranged, 4d4 acid + 2d4 per round).
- **Bonus Action:** Each casts a **damaging cantrip** as bonus action.
- **Movement:** Spread out, maintain distance, all 20+ feet from melee threats.

### **Red Wizards at Spires (5 flying)**
- **Action:** The five aerial wizards sense the Blue head's power. They coordinate a devastating spell volley.
- **Action:** Each casts spells:
  - **Magus Cerulean:** **Chain Lightning** (6d6 lightning, DC 16 DEX save, jumps to nearby targets).
  - **Magus Platinum:** **Cone of Cold** (9d8 cold, DC 16 CON save).
  - **Magus Viridian:** **Poison Spray** OR **Cloudkill** (concentration).
  - **Magus Obsidian II:** **Acid Splash** or **Scorching Burst** (4d6 fire, DC 16 DEX half).
  - **Magus Vermillion:** **Fireball** (10d6 fire, save DC 17, 20-ft. radius).
- **Altitude:** All maintain 60+ feet, raining death from above.

---

**End of Round 7:**
- **Four Heads Attack:** White, Black, Green, Blue bites all land. Expect **60–100 total damage across party**.
- **Breath Weapons:** 2–3 heads use breath weapons. Expect **40–50 damage per target** (many in AoE).
- **Lightning Damage Zone:** **2d6 lightning** for all in 25-ft. radius (DC 13 DEX save).
- **Severin's Status:** **UNKILLABLE** (min. 3 HP).
- **Ritual Check:** DC 16 (was 15, +1 for ritual progress). If successful, ritual advances to Round 8—TIAMAT FULLY MANIFEST.

## 🔴 ROUND 8: RED HEAD EMERGES – TIAMAT FULLY MANIFEST

**Ritual Clock:** 8 / Heads through portal: 5 (ALL - White, Black, Green, Blue, Red) – **TIAMAT COMPLETE**

**Tiamat Status:** The Red head—the largest and most terrible—pushes through the portal with a sound like reality tearing. For the first time, TIAMAT IS FULLY MANIFEST. She can now move freely, act with full initiative, and use all her powers.

## Key Mechanics for DM

### **Ritual Advancement (End of Each Round)**
- **Round 1–3:** DC 12 to advance (party can disrupt easily).
- **Round 4–6:** DC 13–15 (increases each round).
- **Round 7–8:** DC 16+ (nearly impossible to hold).
- **Modify DC:** +1 for each Red Wizard killed. Automatic fail if Mask of the Dragon Queen is destroyed.

### **How the Party Wins**
1. **Kill Severin** (while portal is open) → Portal closes, Tiamat retreats to Avernus.
2. **Destroy all 5 masks** → Tiamat is weakened; battle continues but party has a fighting chance.
3. **Hold out 10+ rounds** → Metallic Dragons + Alliance arrive. Combat shifts to their favor.
4. **Creative solutions** → Collapse temple (Earthquake spell), dispel the ritual with high-level magic, etc.
