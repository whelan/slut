# PLAZA COMPLETE DM GUIDE
## Scenes 3–6: Tunnel Passage → Plaza Crossing → Temple Entry

**For:** Running the party's exit from the warrens to the temple entrance
**Duration:** ~45–60 minutes at the table
**Route:** Area 7 (current) → Area 6 (cleared) → Area 23 Tunnel → Plaza → Temple Front Gate (Blue Chapel, Area 1)

---

## SCENE 3 – Northern Exits Tunnel (Area 23 Entry)

### Setup
- Party emerges from underground into the tunnel mouth
- 2 Barbed Devils + 4 Guard Drakes block the exit
- Devils are watching the plaza (attention divided)
- Guard Drakes are less alert but positioned as backup

### Boxed Text (Dansk)

> Tunnelen stiger opad. Ikke skarpt — bare en lang, glat opadgående bue. Hver hundrede skridt ændrer luften sig fint. Den underjordiske varme aftager. En anden lugt blander sig indmed — aske, blod, og noget der er længere hen på vejen til råddent.
>
> Lyset ændrer sig. Først bliver det bare mindre brunt og mere gråt. Så — så bliver det virkelig — der kommer lys fra uden for. Ikke lys fra en fakkeltrækning. Dagslys. Eller natlys. Du kan ikke helt sige.
>
> Foran jer, oppe ved tunnelens ende, kan I høre stemmer. Ikke de syngende stemmer fra templet. Vagter. Og deres stemmer er på Infernal.

### Discovery: Devils' Common Speech

If **Axar makes Perception DC 12**, he overhears:

> En af djævlene vender sig og snakker til den anden. Hans stemme er som rost metal. Han taler i menneskeligt tungemål — Common, brugt til koordinering med kultister.
>
> *"Severin lovede os blod ved midnat. Ved Tiamat! Tiden er passeret. Hvor er de fanger, han talte om? Vi venter her, og der kommer intet."*
>
> Den anden djævel kalder tilbage: *"Tålmodighed. Ritualet afhænger af timing. De kommer, når Severin er klar. Og så — så får vi hvad vi vil have."*

**CRITICAL:** The sacrifice has NOT started yet. The party knows they have a window.

### Guard Placement

```
Tunnel mouth (50 ft wide)
│
├─ Position A (North): 1 Barbed Devil (AC 15, 110 HP)
├─ Position B (South): 1 Barbed Devil (AC 15, 110 HP)
├─ Position C (Center, 10 ft back): 4 Guard Drakes (AC 12, 52 HP each)
│
└─ Open Plaza (250 ft × 100 ft, bleak, dragon bones)
```

### Combat Options

#### Option A: Group Stealth Check (DC 16)
- If successful: party slips past undetected
- Leads directly to **Scene 4b – The Plaza Sacrifice** (if ritual is active)
- No combat, no sound, no delay
- **Consequence:** Witness the sacrifice ritual in progress

#### Option B: Combat from Behind (Surprise Round)
- 2 Barbed Devils (CR 5 each): 110 HP, Multiattack (tail/claw), Hurl Flame (3d6 fire, 60 ft range)
- 4 Guard Drakes (CR 2 each): 52 HP, Bite + Tail
- **Victory DC:** All enemies dead by Round 3 (sound rule: >3 rounds = dragons hear the fight)
- **Axar Alpha Strike (Fireball):** Kills all drakes (likely), bloodies 1 devil. Extremely LOUD. Dragons notice immediately. Costs surprise round on Blue Chapel wizard (Scene 7).

#### Option C: Twilight Suggestion on a Drake (DC 18 WIS save)
- Removes 1 drake from the fight
- Devils notice and target Twilight in Round 1: *"Magiker!"*
- Combat advantage is marginal; High risk, low reward

#### Option D: Axar Counterspell + Chill Touch
- Mage Armor on self or others
- Counterspell the Barbed Devils' Hurl Flame (if they cast it)
- Drains spell slots but avoids damage

### Sound Rule Reminder
Per `well-of-dragons-raw.md:30`:
- **Combat ≤3 rounds, under 200 ft:** Dragons don't notice
- **Combat >3 rounds:** Dragons hear it. Automatic –1 on next skill check (Scene 6 plaza crossing)
- **Extremely loud noise (Fireball, Thunderwave):** Dragons notice instantly

### Mechanical Updates
- **Ritual clock:** No change from tunnel transit alone
- **Combat duration tracking:** If fight goes >3 rounds, note it for Scene 6
- **Resource tracking:** This is before Long Rest. Watch spell slots and HP carefully.

### DM Notes
- The devils speak **Infernal**. If party has Infernal, they understand. If not, tone is hostile and intention is clear.
- **Devils are not panicked.** They are waiting, confident, cold.
- If party kills the devils silently and exits quickly, they maintain the element of surprise for Scene 4/4b.

## SCENE 4b – THE PLAZA SACRIFICE (Conditional)

### Trigger
**This scene occurs IF:**
1. Party successfully sneaks past tunnel guards (Stealth DC 16), OR
2. Party defeats guards quickly and quietly (combat ≤3 rounds with no extremely loud spells)
3. Party reaches the plaza edge while the **ritual is completing**

**If party fights guards for >3 rounds or uses Fireball:** Skip to Scene 5 (plaza view). Cultists are already alert. No sacrifice scene.

---

### The Five Prisoners

Cultists are chanting. Magus Thezzar stands at the altar, performing the ritual sacrifice. He is taking orders from **Rath Modar**, whose voice echoes from the temple above.

**The Five Prisoners (Named):**

| Name | Background | Death Order | PC Connection | Details |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Stirling** | Red-haired dwarf, guard captain from Baldur's Gate | 1st (first to die) | None (unless DM assigns) | Falls first; loudest scream |
| **Kess** | Young human woman, merchant's daughter | 2nd | None (unless DM assigns) | Captured in Waterdeep 2 months ago; tries to run |
| **Thorne** | Elf ranger, Harper scout | 3rd | Leosin connection (Harper) OR solo discovery | Was surveying the caldera; spits defiance before blade |
| **Marta** | Half-orc farmwoman | 4th | Possibly met in rumors | Captured with family; cries for her children |
| **Fen** | 16-year-old boy | 5th | **Daxx's choice** (Procession scene) | Volunteer from Procession; if NOT saved, dies here chanting ritual words he doesn't understand |

**Alternative for 5th prisoner:** If Fen WAS saved during Procession encounter, the 5th prisoner is a **nameless cultist who broke from mind control** and tried to escape. His sacrifice is punishment.

---

### The Ritual Master: Magus Thezzar

**Stats:**
- AC 15 (Mage Armor)
- HP 40
- Spells: Fireball, Hold Person, Misty Step
- Wears crimson robes with black trim
- Carries obsidian ritual dagger
- Middle-aged human male with acid-burned face (from ritual gone wrong)

**Character:**
- Not the architect, but the executor
- Takes mental commands from **Rath Modar** (ritual link, magical resonance)
- Cold, precise authority
- If killed: ritual coordination breaks momentarily, but Rath can command another wizard to continue

**Rath Modar's Commands (heard by party):**
- *"The blood must flow in sequence, Thezzar."*
- *"Do not rush the fifth. The Queen demands precision."*

This establishes Rath as the true power while keeping him on Level 2 (Red Chapel), not Level 3.

---

### Round-by-Round Action

#### Round 1 – Recognition

> I ser fyrsten af jer kender. Eller måske I ser ham ikke — det er for langt, skyggerne er for mørk — men I ser at nogen der er mennesker bliver til ikke-mennesker. I ser at ritualet ikke længere er abstrakt ord. Det er blod i asken. Det er stilhed efter et menneskes sidste vejr.

**Mechanical Effects:**
- Any PC with personal connection to a prisoner: **WIS save DC 15 or Frightened until end of next turn**
- **Ritual clock +1** (first death)
- All PCs can see the first prisoner die

#### Round 2 – Understanding

The second prisoner dies. Then the third. Then the fourth.

> Den anden bliver dræbt. Og så den tredje. Og så den fjerde. Ikke i slag — I ville kunne modstå det hvis det var kamp. Det er blot dødsfald, efterhånden, som om døden selv er et ritual, en ændring af tilstande, og ingen af dem ville komme ud af det andet sted.
>
> Templet over jer hviner højere.

**Mechanical Effects:**
- **Ritual clock +1 per death** (so +3 total after deaths 2, 3, 4)
- **If Fen is in the circle:** Daxx must make **WIS save DC 16 or be Stunned 1 round** (cannot move or take actions; helpless)
- **Frygtløs reaction:** May trigger Barbarian Rage automatically if he recognizes a martyr or feels the injustice

#### Round 3 – The Final Death

The fifth prisoner is brought forward.

> Den sidste — manden eller drengen eller hvem han var før ritualet havde hævn på ham — løftes til alteret. Og Rath Modar råber noget på et språk I ikke forstår, men hvis betydning I forstår perfekt.
>
> Drengens øjne åbner sig for første gang.
>
> Han ser jer.
>
> Og så bliver der ikke mere syn.

**Mechanical Effects:**
- **Ritual clock +1** (now +5 total if all die uninterrupted)
- **All PCs:** **WIS save DC 16 or take 1 level of Exhaustion** (horror of witnessing)
- **If Daxx was stunned in Round 2:** He is free on his next turn

---

### Party Response: Rescue Attempt (Optional)

If party attempts to interrupt the ritual BEFORE all 5 deaths:

#### Rescue Check: DC 16 Acrobatics
- Reach the altar before Thezzar strikes the next prisoner
- Success: **One prisoner can be unbound and escape** toward temple or back to tunnel
- Clock impact: **–1** (cancels 1 death's clock advancement)

#### Combat Eruption
If party initiates combat on the plaza:
- **Magus Thezzar** engages with Fireball, Hold Person, Misty Step
- **15+ cultists** (Dragonfang, Dragonwing, Red Wizard support) converge on the party
- **Difficulty:** Hard fight, 15+ enemies, no cover, open ground
- **Rath Modar's response (from temple):** *"KILL THEM, THEZZAR! THE QUEEN'S HUNGER CANNOT WAIT!"*
- **Ritual impact:** If <5 wizards chant for 2 consecutive rounds, portal collapses (clock resets to 0 — massive weakening)

#### Stealth Approach
- **Contested Perception vs. Party Stealth** (DC 16+) to reach rescue position without alerting cultists
- Cultists in ritual trance; most won't notice if party avoids line-of-sight
- Thezzar is focused on the sacrifice; less attentive to surroundings

---

### Axar's Arcana Opportunity

As the ritual completes, **Axar makes Arcana check DC 16:**

**Success:**
- Understands the ritual geometry: **Five deaths = five foci being awakened simultaneously**
- Realizes: **If party sabotages the foci on Level 2, they can partially undo this work**
- Key insight: **Rath Modar is channeling this ritual from the temple above** (voice = direct magical link)
- Conclusion: **Rath is on Level 2 (Red Chapel), not Level 3 with Severin**
- **Mechanical payoff:** –1 to ritual clock at Level 2 Foci encounter (one sabotage is "free")

**Failure:**
- Sees the power but not the structure
- Ritual is complete; doesn't realize Rath's location or the foci connection

---

### Cultist Reaction (Post-Ritual)

**The cultists DO NOT immediately see the party.** They are deep in ritual trance. But if party makes ANY sound or movement:
- **Drake Perception DC 12:** They notice
- Cultists will converge to **protect the ritual** from perceived disruption
- **Rath Modar will NOT leave the altar** — he must complete the final channeling

**If cultists are alerted but party still reaches the temple:**
- Plaza crossing (Scene 6) becomes **hot landing zone** instead of stealth challenge
- Cultists converge as party moves toward temple; combat may erupt mid-crossing
- Party loses surprise round on Blue Chapel wizard (Scene 7)

---

### Aftermath of Scene 4b

Once the sacrifice completes (all 5 dead or party intervenes):
- **Ritual clock is locked at +5** (worst case) or **+2 to +3** (if party rescued prisoners)
- The temple above goes quiet for a moment
- **Rath's voice from the temple:** *"It is done. The Queen's hunger is sated. For now."*
- Cultists remain in position, waiting for next command

Party **must now cross the plaza** to reach the temple entrance (Scene 6).

## SCENE 5 – Emerging Into the Plaza (First View of Temple)

### Boxed Text (Dansk)

> I træder ud af tunnelen, og verden åbner sig over jer som en sår i klippen.
>
> Calderaen omslutter jer som en kæmpe skål af sort sten — en vulkans døde hjerte. Bunden er dækket af drageknogler: ribben på størrelse med bjælker, kæbeben som heste-trug, et kranie der står midt i plazaen som en enkelt stille øje, stirrende mod templet. Og der ligger flere — tusinder af dem — rullet sammen som høje efter høst.
>
> Plazaen selv er ryddet af knogler — en lang, åben kløft der strækker sig 250 fod foran jer og ind mod templet, og det er så åbent, det føles som at gå på is.
>
> Og templet.
>
> Det er en sindssyg struktur, der har trængt sig op fra under knoglerne for at tårne sig over den brændte vulkan. Vulkansk aske smeltet sammen med drageknogler, sten der er gennemsyret af Helvedes magi. Det er fuld af kaotiske vinkler og udragende støttepiller. Og der i det hele, i dets vrede af kornicher og himmelstræbende vinkler, ser I dem — fem tårne, hver toppet af et snoet spir.
>
> Blå. Hvid. Grøn. Rød. Sort.
>
> Hvert spir er som en knyttet næve, der rækker op mod en himmel, der ikke ønsker det.
>
> Højt over jer kredser drager. I tæller dem ikke — I lader være. I ved bare, at I aldrig har set så mange chromatic drager på samme tid, levende og vrede og *venlige* i deres veje mod himlen. De skrika til hinanden. De udgør et bulldrende spektakel, hvor hundreder af stemmer kalder hver anden: *"Hun kommer! Hun kommer! Hun kommer!"*

### Per-PC First Notice

- **Axar**: The temple's structure is *wrong*. It violates geometry he knows. The five spires positioned as in harmony, but the chords they make are off. Singing something like prayer but tasting like pain.
- **Daxx**: The dragons. Dozens. Circling, waiting, excited but undisciplined. Not hunting the party — not yet. But they could, if the wind changed.
- **Frygtløs**: The *scale*. Open air after hours underground. Freedom to move. But also: the pressure of being seen. Dragons above. Temple pushes against sky.
- **Twilight**: The dragon-hymn — beautiful and horrible. Harmony underneath. A song she's never heard. Something in her wants to answer.

### DM Technique
**After reading this boxed text, WAIT at least 20 seconds in silence.** Let players look. Let them describe what they see. Absorb the *scale*. Do not move on until someone breaks the silence.

## SCENE 6 – Crossing the Plaza (Skill Challenge)

### Framework

**Distance:** 250 feet of open ground
**Enemies above:** Chromatic dragons circling
**Condition:** 4 successes = safe passage / 2 failures = dragon notice

The party rolls skills as they cross. Each success = progress. Each failure = dragon notice risk.

### Success/Failure Outcomes

#### Full Success (4 successes, 0–1 failures)
> I bevæger jer i etaper. Knogle til knogle. Hver gang en drageskygge går hen over jeres positioner, fryser I. Ikke bevægelse. Ikke åndedræt. Selv jeres hjerte synes at stoppe.
>
> Når skyggen passerer, løber I videre.
>
> Det tager mindst tyve minutter at krydse plazaen. Tyve minutter af at trække vejret gennem åben mund for ikke at lave en lyd. Tyve minutter af at se templet vokse med hver skridt.
>
> Da I når frem til dets skygge, er I ikke længere bange. I er bare meget meget træt på en måde, der ikke er fysisk.
>
> Ingen drager har set jer.

**Result:** Reach temple front gate undetected. Maintain surprise round on Blue Chapel Red Wizard (Scene 7).

#### Partial Success (4 successes, 2 failures)
> I bevæger jer i etaper. Knogle til knogle. Det virker.
>
> Og så — halvvejs over plazaen — en ung rød drages øjne rammer plads hvor I *var* en sekund tidligere. Han skrigter — ikke en ord, bare en råben der genlyder gennem hele calderaen.
>
> De andre drager vender sig. De se ikke jer. Men de ser efter jer.
>
> I løber de sidste 125 fod. Ikke stilhed længere. Bare bevægelse.
>
> I når templets dør med dragernes hyl i øret. Ingen har fulgt. Endnu.

**Result:** Reach temple gate, but lose surprise round on Blue Chapel wizard. He is alert and ready to cast.

#### Catastrophic (Failed attempt, <4 successes or 2+ failures)
> En voksen grøn drages udbreder sine vinger og dykker. Den er *stor* — størrere end I er høj.
>
> **DC 16 Dexterity saving throw.** Succes: 6d10 acid halved. Fejl: fuld skade, og I er dumpet bagover af slagkraften.
>
> Dragen passerer over jer som en stormvind. Den *kunne* have slået hovedet af. Det gjorde den ikke. Det var en advarsel.
>
> Dragen stiger igen og vender tilbage til sine søstre. Og den lyder som om, den le.

**Result:** One dragon intercepts. All PCs make DEX save DC 16. Takes 6d10 acid damage (halved on success). Dragon withdraws. No TPK (Tiamat will not allow interference — yet). Surprise round is definitely lost.

### Skill Options (DC 15 each)

| Skill | Approach | PC | Modifiers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stealth** | Bone pile to bone pile, broken-line approach | Daxx | +advantage if Cloak active |
| **Survival** | Time dragon flight patterns, read rhythm | Daxx | Expertise applies |
| **Arcana** | Cast Pass Without Trace, protect aura | Axar | Party +10 to Stealth if cast before |
| **Performance** | Mimic cultist signal, drag illusory prisoner | Twilight | Disguise Self as support |
| **Athletics** | Sprint section, distraction | Frygtløs | High risk, one success only |

### Critical Resource: Pass Without Trace

If **Daxx casts Pass Without Trace** (2nd-level Ranger spell) before plaza crossing:
- **All party members within 30 ft gain +10 to Stealth**
- DC 15 becomes auto-success for Daxx, Twilight
- **Very likely success** for Frygtløs and Axar
- **This is the single best resource allocation for Scene 6**

## SCENE 7 – Temple Front Gate (Blue Chapel, Area 1)

### Setup
- Red Wizard standing alone at the entrance
- Back to party, facing altar of blue crystal
- Chanting ritual words

### Boxed Text (Dansk)

> Templets indgang er en bue af sort sten, sammenflettet med drageknogler. Der er ingen dør. Indgangen er åben — som et munde, der inviterer, eller som en fælde.
>
> Indenfor: en figur i rød kappe. Han står med ryggen til jer, foran et alter af blå krystal. Hans hænder er løftet mod det indre af templet. Hans stemme rejser sig i en tone, I ikke har hørt et menneske lave før — ikke helt ord, ikke helt musik, snarere musiklens skelet.
>
> Han har ikke set jer. Han er helt og holdent optaget af det, han synger.
>
> Hans kappes manchet er broderet med symboler: det Thayanske Brennemærke — flammer omkring en tredobbelt pyramid.

### NPC: Veksin Modar (Blue Chapel Red Wizard)

**Name:** Veksin Modar (Thayan; distant relative of Rath Modar)

**Stats:**
- AC 15 (Mage Armor)
- HP 40
- Spells prepared: Cone of Cold, Fireball, Hold Person, Misty Step
- 3rd-level Wizard

**Character Details:**
- Age: 24 years old
- Appearance: Scar on left cheek (from ritual gone wrong, teaching incident)
- Wears: Crimson robes with black trim
- Carries: Silver ring with child's name engraved inside — *Vestin* (his son, left in Thay)
- Background: Youngest of Rath's apprentices; humming a melody his mother sang

### Per-PC First Notice

- **Axar**: Veksin's posture — not a warrior's stance, but someone *holding something*. He is not the ritual's leader; he is a vessel.
- **Daxx**: His breathing. Steady. Prepared. He has been standing here for hours and is *ready* for the end.
- **Frygtløs**: The scar on his left cheek — a wound, healed but visible. Frygtløs recognizes wound-craft. This man has been *hurt*.
- **Twilight**: The humming. She knows that melody. Her mother sang it. *She's* been humming it since they left area 7. This young man is humming it too, like a prayer.

### Combat Options

#### Option 1: Silent Kill (Surprise Round)
- **Group Stealth DC 14** (drops to DC 16 if plaza crossing failed)
- If successful: surprise round on Veksin. Party acts first.
- **Mechanical payoff:** If Veksin dies in surprise without raising alarm (no spell cast to alert temple), **4 Red Wizards remain active**. If party can keep <5 wizards chanting for **2 successive rounds**, portal collapses and ritual clock resets to 0 (major weakening event).

**Veksin's last moment (silent):**
> Hans hånd falder. Hans stemme kuttet af. Humningen stopper. Hans ring glider af og falder til gulvet — du ser navnet indeni: *Vestin*.

#### Option 2: Loud Combat (Initiative Round 1)
- Veksin reacts to attack. Initiative.
- Round 1: Veksin uses Cone of Cold if 2+ enemies within 15 ft.
- Round 2: Other 4 Red Wizards notice the disruption. Rath Modar feels it and begins ascent to intercept (arrives Round 3).

**Veksin's last moment (loud):**
> Han dør i en glimtvis af blå kulde eller ild. Ingen sidste ord. Bare: væk.

#### Option 3: Twilight Suggestion (DC 18 WIS save)
- Twilight casts Suggestion: *"Step out for a moment. The ritual can wait."*
- If he fails: he walks out confused and docile
- **Mechanical payoff:** Keeps surprise round BUT **does NOT disrupt ritual count** (5 wizards still active)
- **Veksin's moment:** He walks out confused: *"Rath Modar, the air — something is wrong —"* Then silence.

### The Humanization Beat (Frygtløs's Naergoth Parallel)

**This is Frygtløs's second "should I swing?" moment.**

Veksin is:
- Young (24)
- Afraid (evident in body language)
- A father separated from his son (ring detail)
- Humming a song of home (melody connection)
- Unaware the party is there

**DM technique:** Do not ask Frygtløs directly. Describe Veksin's vulnerability. Let him decide. Silence is the reward either way — no judgment, no praise.

This mirrors his choice with Naergoth at area 7 entrance. That time: he swung first. This time: will he?
