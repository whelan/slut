The 10-Round Ritual Breakdown assumes the party is already inside the main cathedral space by Round 1. **It does NOT account for:**

1. **Magus Azuri as immediate threat** when party enters Area 1
2. **Phased entry** (party must cross from chapel vault → main cathedral)

**What the party hears/sees:**
- A loud explosion or electrical burst echoing through the temple.
- Rath Modar immediately becomes aware: "INFILTRATORS IN AREA ONE!"

- The ritual's rhythm is disrupted (Azuri is no longer chanting).

**Ritual effect:**

- **Ritual Check fails** at end of Round 1 if Azuri is still not chanting.
- **Clock does NOT advance** if Azuri is killed before Round 1 ends.

**Call out:**

Ahead, the cathedral-like interior of Tiamat's Temple yawns before you—five vaults in different colors surround a massive central apse where a portal of swirling energy pulses. Five Red Wizards stand in each vault, chanting in unison. Above, five more Red Wizards hover near the spires. And at the very top... Severin, his form haloed in eldritch light, arms raised to the portal.

> A Red Wizard in the Blue Chapel—directly ahead of you—turns. His eyes widen in recognition of your faces. He begins to speak."

**That Red Wizard is Magus Azuri.** He will:
1. **Action 1:** Cast a loud spell (Fireball toward area outside chapel, or shout in Infernal to alert others).

3. **Movement:** Try to reach the main cathedral to link with other wizards or create distance.

## REVISED ROUNDS 1–3 (Blue Chapel Entry)

### **ROUND 1: Ambush in the Blue Chapel**

**Magus Azuri (Blue Chapel, Area 1)**
- **Action:** Casts **Fireball** (8d6 fire, DC 17 save, 20-ft. radius) toward the main cathedral entrance as a warning/attack.
- **Bonus Action:** Moves 30 feet toward the main cathedral opening, creating line of sight with Rath Modar.
- **Reaction:** Ready Counterspell against disruptive spells.
- **Status:** No longer chanting. Ritual loses this wizard's focus.

**Rath Modar (Red Chapel, Area 6)**
- **Action:** Hears/sees Azuri's Fireball. Casts **Counterspell** (reaction ready) to cancel any party dispel magic.
- **Bonus Action:** Moves 20 feet toward the main cathedral (watching for party to emerge).
- **Concentration:** Maintains **Globe of Invulnerability** (already active, pre-combat).
- **Status:** Still chanting partially; aware of infiltrators; ready for combat.

**Severin (Sanctuary, Area 13)**
- **Action:** Continues ritual chanting; hears distant Fireball but does not directly see it.
- **Bonus Action – Ritual Action:** Clock +1 (advances to Round 1). **RITUAL FAILS IF AZURI DOES NOT REJOIN** – only 4 of 5 minimum wizards are chanting.

**Flying Wizards (5 spires)**
- **Status:** Hear the Fireball; alert posture. No visual on party yet (blocked by vault architecture).
- **Action:** Ready offensive spells, prepare to swoop down if party enters main cathedral.

**Ritual Check (End of Round 1):**
- **Only 4 Red Wizards actively chanting** (Alabaster, Verdian, Obsidian, + Rath partially)
- **Requirement: 5 minimum**
- **Result:** Ritual STALLS. Clock does NOT advance to Round 2 unless Azuri returns to chanting OR another wizard takes his place.

### **ROUND 2: Azuri Falls or Returns**

**Scenario A: Azuri is killed in combat**
- Ritual completely fails (only 3 wizards left: Alabaster, Verdian, Obsidian, Rath partially)
- Clock resets to 0 if stalled for 2 consecutive rounds
- Party has successfully disrupted the ritual from the start

**Scenario B: Azuri retreats to main cathedral**
- **Azuri moves** 30 feet into the main apse area
- **New line of sight:** Flying wizards can now see Azuri fleeing party
- **Flying wizards take action:** Cast offensive spells toward the party in the chapel vault (not at Azuri)
- **Azuri reaches ritual focus area:** Rejoins chanting. Ritual recovers (now 5+ wizards)

**Magus Azuri (if retreating)**
- **Action:** Moves into the main cathedral; begins chanting again while standing near Rath Modar
- **Bonus Action:** Casts **Mirror Image** or **Shield of Faith** for defense
- **Status:** Now part of the defensive ritual group; no longer in direct combat with party in the vault

**Rath Modar**
- **Action:** Prepares **Fireball** or **Counterspell** against party spells
- **Bonus Action:** Ritual focus; chants alongside Azuri (maintaining 5+ wizard requirement)
- **Reaction:** Ready to Counterspell disruptive magic

**Flying Wizards**
- **Action:** Cast offensive spells NOW that they have line of sight into the main cathedral:
  - **Magus Cerulean:** Lightning Bolt (4d6, DC 16 DEX save)
  - **Magus Platinum:** Cone of Cold (6d8, DC 16 CON save)
  - **Magus Viridian:** Insect Plague (concentration, 4d10 piercing)
  - **Magus Obsidian II:** Scorching Burst (3d6 fire)
  - **Magus Vermillion:** Fireball (8d6 fire, DC 17 save)
- **Altitude:** Stay 50+ feet above party; maintain aerial advantage

**Severin (Sanctuary)**
- **Action:** Ritual chanting continues
- **Bonus Action – Ritual Action:** Clock +1 IF ritual resumed (if 5+ wizards are chanting)
- **Status:** Aware of threat below; continues ritual; does not directly engage yet

**Ritual Check (End of Round 2):**
- If Azuri survived and returned: **5+ wizards chanting** → Clock advances to Round 2 ✓
- If Azuri is dead and party hasn't reached main cathedral: **4 wizards only** → Clock STALLS; party has disrupted opening phase successfully

---

### **ROUND 3: Party Reaches Main Cathedral**

**Party has now crossed from Blue Chapel vault into the main apse area (Area 7)**

**All NPCs now have clear line of sight and direct engagement:**

**Magus Azuri (if alive, main cathedral)**
- **Action:** Continues combat or ritual chanting (depends if party is still engaging him)
- **Bonus Action:** Casts offensive spell OR assists with ritual focus

**Rath Modar (Red Chapel, Area 6)**
- **Action:** Casts **Wall of Fire** or **Confusion** (offensive magic)
- **Bonus Action:** Ritual focus (maintains chanting)
- **Movement:** May move 30 feet closer to central apse for better positioning
- **Status:** NOW fully engaged in combat; Ritual duties secondary

**Flying Wizards (5)**
- **Action:** Full offensive barrage (all cast high-level spells toward party):
  - Lightning Bolt, Cone of Cold, Cloudkill, Scorching Burst, Fireball
  - Targeting lowest-HP party members
- **Altitude:** Maintain 50+ feet above (out of melee range, mostly safe)

**Severin (Sanctuary)**
- **Action:** Ritual chanting
- **Bonus Action – Ritual Action:** Clock +1 (advances to Round 3)
- **Reaction:** Ready to Counterspell or defend masks if party attempts direct approach
- **Status:** Now aware party has reached main cathedral; concentrating harder on ritual

**Ritual Check (End of Round 3):**
- **5+ wizards chanting** (if Azuri survived; if dead, only 4 → ritual stalls again)
- **Success:** Clock advances to Round 3. Party has "disruption window" closing fast.
- **Failure:** Clock resets to 0 if 2 rounds stalled in a row.
