## MAGUS THALLID THE SHADOWBORN

Medium or Small Humanoid (Wizard), Chaotic Evil

**AC** 15 **Initiative** +2  
**HP** 71 (16d8)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| MOD | SAVE | | MOD | SAVE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| STR 9 | -1 | -1 | INT 19 | +4 | +6 |
| DEX 14 | +2 | +2 | WIS 12 | +1 | +3 |
| CON 11 | +0 | +0 | CHA 11 | +0 | +0 |

**Skills** Arcana +6, History +6, Perception +3  
**Gear** Wand of the Warmage, Robe of the Archmagi  
**Treasure** Arcana, Individual  
**Environment** Temple, Ritual Chamber  
**Senses** Passive Perception 13  
**Languages** Common, Thayan, Draconic  
**CR** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)

### Traits

**Spellcasting.** Thallid casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):
- *At Will:* Detect Magic, Light, Mage Armor (included in AC), Mage Hand, Prestidigitation
- *2/Day Each:* Fireball (cast at 4th level), Invisibility
- *1/Day Each:* Cone of Cold, Fly, Counterspell

**Fire Resistance.** Thallid has resistance to fire damage (gift of Tiamat).

**Thayan Evocation Mastery.** When Thallid casts a damaging spell of 2nd level or higher, he can choose one creature within 30 feet to have resistance to the spell's damage type until the end of his next turn.

**Ritual Focus (3/Day).** When Thallid casts a spell as part of a ritual that requires concentration, he can maintain concentration for 1 additional minute beyond the spell's duration.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Thallid makes three Arcane Burst attacks.

**Arcane Burst.** *Melee or Ranged Spell Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d8 + 3) force damage.

**Spellcasting.** Thallid casts one of the spells listed under Spellcasting (above), using the same spellcasting ability.

**Fireburst (Recharge 5–6).** Thallid casts Fireball at 4th-level, centered on himself. Each creature that fails the save takes 22 (5d8) fire damage instead of full damage.

### Bonus Actions

**Misty Step (3/Day).** Thallid casts Misty Step, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

### Reactions

**Protective Magic (3/Day).** Thallid casts Counterspell or Shield in response to the spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

**Evocation Strike.** When a creature within 60 feet makes an attack roll against Thallid, he can use his reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack, but must use Protective Magic before the result of the attack is determined.

### Treasure

**Wand of the Warmage** (Rare, requires attunement)
- +1 to spell attack rolls
- Can add 1d4 force damage to spells that deal damage

**Robe of the Archmagi** (Very Rare, requires attunement)
- +2 to AC (included above)
- +2 to spell save DC (included above)
- Wearer can recover one expended spell slot per day (max 6th level)

## MAGUS SZASS THE DEFILER

Medium or Small Humanoid (Wizard), Chaotic Evil

**AC** 15 **Initiative** +2  
**HP** 82 (18d8)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| MOD | SAVE | | MOD | SAVE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| STR 9 | -1 | -1 | INT 20 | +5 | +7 |
| DEX 14 | +2 | +2 | WIS 12 | +1 | +4 |
| CON 11 | +0 | +0 | CHA 11 | +0 | +0 |

**Skills** Arcana +7, History +7, Insight +4, Perception +4  
**Gear** Wand of Fireballs, Robe of the Archmagi, Ring of Protection  
**Treasure** Arcana, Paragon  
**Environment** Temple, Ritual Chamber  
**Senses** Passive Perception 14  
**Languages** Common, Thayan, Draconic  
**CR** 6 (XP 2,300; PB +3)

### Traits

**Spellcasting.** Szass casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):
- *At Will:* Detect Magic, Light, Mage Armor (included in AC), Mage Hand, Prestidigitation
- *2/Day Each:* Fireball (cast at 4th level), Invisibility, Contingency
- *1/Day Each:* Cone of Cold, Fly, Counterspell, Telekinesis

**Fire Resistance.** Szass has resistance to fire damage (gift of Tiamat).

**Ritual Mastery.** Szass has advantage on concentration checks related to ritual magic. When Szass attempts to maintain ritual focus, the spell's duration extends by 1 minute. Szass can use Counterspell to defend or advance draconic rituals as a bonus action (3/Day).

**Legendary Spellcasting.** Szass can cast a cantrip as a bonus action on each turn.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Szass makes three Arcane Burst attacks.

**Arcane Burst.** *Melee or Ranged Spell Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d8 + 3) force damage.

**Spellcasting.** Szass casts one of the spells listed under Spellcasting (above), using the same spellcasting ability.

**Transmutation Strike (Recharge 5–6).** Szass casts Cone of Cold or Fireball, centered up to 120 feet away. Each creature in the area must make an Intelligence saving throw (DC 15), taking 27 (6d8) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

**Wand Strike (1/Day).** Szass uses the Wand of Fireballs to cast Fireball without using a spell slot. If used this way, the DC is 15.

### Bonus Actions

**Misty Step (3/Day).** Szass casts Misty Step, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

**Ritual Intervention (1/Day).** When another wizard within 60 feet fails a concentration check for ritual magic, Szass can use his bonus action to grant advantage on that check.

### Reactions

**Protective Magic (3/Day).** Szass casts Counterspell or Shield in response to the spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

**Ritual Counterspell (3/Day).** When a creature within 60 feet casts a spell that would disrupt draconic ritual magic, Szass can use his reaction to cast Counterspell with advantage on the check.

### Treasure

**Wand of Fireballs** (Rare)
- Contains 7 charges; regains 1d6 + 1 at dawn
- *Fireball (DC 15).* As an action, expend 1 charge to cast Fireball (4th level), centered on a point within 120 feet

**Robe of the Archmagi** (Very Rare, requires attunement)
- +2 to AC (included above)
- +3 to spell save DC (included above)
- Resistance to spell damage
- Recover one expended spell slot per day (max 6th level)

**Ring of Protection** (Rare, requires attunement)
- +1 to AC (already included in AC 15)
- +1 to all saving throws (included above)

## MAGUS ZALATHORM THE DRACONIC

Medium or Small Humanoid (Wizard), Chaotic Evil

**AC** 15 **Initiative** +2  
**HP** 71 (16d8)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| MOD | SAVE | | MOD | SAVE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| STR 9 | -1 | -1 | INT 19 | +4 | +6 |
| DEX 14 | +2 | +2 | WIS 12 | +1 | +3 |
| CON 11 | +0 | +0 | CHA 11 | +0 | +0 |

**Skills** Arcana +6, History +6, Insight +3, Perception +3  
**Gear** Wand of Secrets, Robe of the Archmagi, Staff of Fire  
**Treasure** Arcana, Paragon  
**Environment** Temple, Ritual Chamber, Scrying Room  
**Senses** Passive Perception 13  
**Languages** Common, Thayan, Draconic, Primordial  
**CR** 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)

### Traits

**Spellcasting.** Zalathorm casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):
- *At Will:* Detect Magic, Light, Mage Armor (included in AC), Mage Hand, Prestidigitation, True Strike
- *2/Day Each:* Invisibility, Scrying (no components required)
- *1/Day Each:* Cone of Cold, Fly, Counterspell, Legend Lore, True Seeing

**Fire Resistance.** Zalathorm has resistance to fire damage (gift of Tiamat).

**Divination Mastery.** Zalathorm has advantage on Intelligence (Arcana) checks related to magical knowledge, dragon lore, and ritual magic. Zalathorm can cast Identify at will (via Wand of Secrets).

**Draconic Insight (1/Day).** Zalathorm can cast Divination without using a spell slot or material components to gain knowledge about draconic or ritual magic within 30 miles.

**Arcane Sense.** When a creature Zalathorm can see casts a spell within 60 feet, Zalathorm can use his reaction to learn the spell being cast and its spell level (if the spell is on the wizard spell list).

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Zalathorm makes three Arcane Burst attacks.

**Arcane Burst.** *Melee or Ranged Spell Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d8 + 3) force damage.

**Spellcasting.** Zalathorm casts one of the spells listed under Spellcasting (above), using the same spellcasting ability.

**Scrying Pulse (Recharge 5–6).** Zalathorm casts Scrying, centered on a point he can see within 120 feet. Each creature in a 20-foot radius must make an Intelligence saving throw (DC 14). On a failed save, a creature is frightened until the end of its next turn and takes 11 (2d10) psychic damage.

**Staff Strike (1/Day).** Zalathorm uses the Staff of Fire to add 2d6 fire damage to a spell or Arcane Burst attack.

### Bonus Actions

**Misty Step (3/Day).** Zalathorm casts Misty Step, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

**Arcane Knowledge (1/Day).** Zalathorm uses his Draconic Insight to gain intelligence about his surroundings or threats within 1 mile.

### Reactions

**Protective Magic (3/Day).** Zalathorm casts Counterspell or Shield in response to the spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

**Arcane Countering (Passive).** When Zalathorm uses Arcane Sense to identify a spell being cast, he can cast Counterspell with advantage on the check.

### Treasure

**Wand of Secrets** (Very Rare, requires attunement)
- Can cast Identify at will
- Contains 3 charges; regains 1d3 at dawn
- *True Seeing (Costs 2 charges).* As an action, cast True Seeing (spell save DC 14) on yourself or another willing creature

**Robe of the Archmagi** (Very Rare, requires attunement)
- +2 to AC (included above)
- +2 to spell save DC (included above)
- Truesight within 120 feet
- Recover one expended spell slot per day (max 6th level)

**Staff of Fire** (Rare, requires attunement)
- Can add 2d6 fire damage to spell attacks made with spells
- Contains 3 charges; regains 1d3 at dawn
- *Flaming Burst (Costs 1 charge).* As an action, cast Fireball (DC 14, 4th level)

## Combat Summary

**Thallid:** Offensive caster focused on area damage. Uses Fireball/Cone of Cold with Evocation Mastery to control battlefield.

**Szass:** Ritual specialist who maintains ritual while defending. Uses Ritual Counterspell to protect magical ceremonies.

**Zalathorm:** Intelligence gatherer who counters enemy spells. Uses Scrying and Arcane Sense for information advantage.

---

## Using These in Combat

**Solo:** CR 6 Szass = Deadly threat  
**Pairs:** 2 CR 5 wizards = Deadly+  
**With Minions:** 1 elite + 4-6 CR 1/8 minions = Challenging+

All three can be used in Temple of Tiamat encounters with different roles.
