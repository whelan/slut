## MAGUS THALLID THE SHADOWBORN
*Medium humanoid (Human, wizard), chaotic evil*

**Armor Class** 15 (Mage Armor)  
**Hit Points** 71 (16d8)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 (-1) | 14 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 19 (+4) | 12 (+1) | 11 (+0) |

**Saving Throws** INT +6, WIS +3  
**Skills** Arcana +6, History +6, Perception +3  
**Senses** passive Perception 13  
**Languages** Common, Thayan, Draconic  
**CR** 5 (1,800 XP; PB +3)

### Special Traits

**Spellcasting.** The wizard casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):
- *At Will:* Detect Magic, Light, Mage Armor (included in AC), Mage Hand, Prestidigitation
- *2/Day Each:* Fireball, Invisibility
- *1/Day Each:* Cone of Cold, Fly, Counterspell

**Fire Resistance.** The wizard has resistance to fire damage, a gift from Tiamat's influence.

**Thayan Ritual Mastery.** The wizard has advantage on concentration checks related to ritual magic and draconic summoning.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** The wizard makes three Arcane Burst attacks.

**Arcane Burst.** *Melee or Ranged Spell Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d8 + 3) force damage.

**Arcane Volley (Recharge 5–6).** The wizard casts Fireball, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

### Bonus Actions

**Misty Step (3/Day).** The wizard casts Misty Step, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

### Reactions

**Protective Magic (3/Day).** The wizard casts Counterspell or Shield in response to the spell's trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

### Treasure (Arcana)

**Wand of the Warmage** (Rare, requires attunement)
- +1 spell attack rolls
- 3 charges/day for +1d4 force damage on spell attacks

**Robe of the Archmagi** (Very Rare, requires attunement)
- +2 AC (included above)
- +2 spell save DC
- Can reduce spell slot cost by 1 level once/day

## Combat Tactics

### Thallid (CR 5) - Evocation Specialist
1. **Opening:** Cast Invisibility to position safely
2. **Main Phase:** Multiattack Arcane Burst (3 attacks per turn)
3. **Escalation:** Use Recharge Arcane Volley (Fireball) on groups
4. **Defense:** Counterspell/Shield reaction; Misty Step to reposition

### Szass (CR 6) - Ritual Master
1. **Ritual Priority:** Maintain ritual advantage on concentration
2. **Combat:** Fireball or Cone of Cold for crowd control
3. **Support:** Use Ritual Intervention to help other ritual casters
4. **Escape:** Misty Step and Invisibility when threatened

### Zalathorm (CR 5) - Divination Expert
1. **Preparation:** Use Draconic Insight to learn party capabilities
2. **Intelligence:** Cast Scrying to track party movements
3. **Countering:** Use Counterspell preemptively (knows spells being cast)
4. **Mobility:** Misty Step to maintain distance

## Encounter Design

### Single Elite Red Wizard Encounter
- **1 CR 5 Red Wizard** + 6-8 Red Wizard minions (CR 1/8) = Challenging (level 15 party)
- **1 CR 6 Red Wizard** + support = Hard

### Multiple Elite Wizards
- **2 CR 5 wizards** = Deadly
- **1 CR 5 + 1 CR 6** = Deadly+
- Use different tactics (one focuses ritual, one focuses combat)

### During Ritual Combat
- **Role:** Maintain ritual DC while defending themselves
- **Counterspell:** Used to counter disruption spells
- **Fireball:** Used on grouped party members
- **Misty Step:** Escape when below 40 HP

## How to Adjust Difficulty

**Easier (CR 4):**
- Reduce HP to 60
- Spell DC 13
- Multiattack: 2 Arcane Burst instead of 3

**Harder (CR 7):**
- Increase HP to 99
- Add 4th level spells (Polymorph, Greater Invisibility)
- Grant Legendary Resistance 1/Day

## Using These in Temple of Tiamat

1. **Ritual Encounter:** 1 CR 5-6 wizard + 3-4 CR 1/8 minions maintaining ritual
2. **Solo Boss:** CR 6 Szass as encounter centerpiece
3. **Support:** CR 5 wizards assist Rath Modar or other antagonists
4. **Paired:** 2 CR 5 wizards working together for Deadly encounter

All three can be found in different temple areas, representing the hierarchy of Red Wizard power.
