| 5 Colored Dragons (Manifestations) | Lair Bosses | Enemy (Legendary) | Sky above plaza | Shadow dragons, linked to ritual clock |

**Key Mechanics:**
- **Ritual Clock progression:** Each sacrifice = Clock +1
- **Party intervention:** Can attempt to rescue prisoners before death
- **Prisoner rescue mechanics:**
  - Stirling: Magical chains, DC 14 STR to break or Dispel Magic
  - Kess: Regular ropes, DC 10 STR (or Sleight of Hand DC 13 for her)
  - Thorne: Magical chains, DC 15 STR or Dispel Magic DC 12
  - Marta: Regular ropes, DC 11 STR
  - Fen: Psychological (not magically bound), needs DC 13 Persuasion or mind-breaking spell
  
**Optional Timing:** Occurs only if party specifically chooses stealth path. Can be skipped if party takes direct approach.

**Post-Rescue:** Rescued prisoners can join as allies for Level 3 (weakened state, gradually regain capability)

- Legendary Resistance 3/Day

- Use to escalate combat pressure as ritual progresses

#### **Clock 8: Full Tiamat Manifestation (Combat Replacement)**
| NPC | Role | Type | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tiamat** (Full form) | Ultimate Boss | Enemy (Legendary) | If Clock reaches 8 | CR 30, Gargantuan fiend, 615 HP, 5 breath weapons, regeneration 30/turn |

**Full Tiamat Combat Features:**
- 5 independent heads (5 reactions/turn)
- 5 bite attacks per round (with elemental damage each)
- 5 breath weapons (2 legendary actions each):
  - Black: 120 ft line, 15d8 acid (DC 27)
  - Blue: 120 ft line, 16d10 lightning (DC 27)
  - Green: 90 ft cone, 22d6 poison (DC 27)
  - Red: 90 ft cone, 26d6 fire (DC 27)
  - White: 90 ft cone, 16d8 cold (DC 27)
- Frightful Presence: DC 26 WIS save, 240 ft radius
- Multiple Heads reaction trait
- Limited Magic Immunity
- Discorporation (can reform if escaped)

**Purpose:**
- Severin is the narrative focus (defeat him to stop ritual)
- Tiamat manifestation is the mechanical pressure
- Combat can be won by:
  1. Defeating Severin (ritual stops, Tiamat fades)
  2. Destroying all 5 masks Severin wears (+10 HP foci)
  3. Surviving until Tiamat fully manifests, then _defeating_ Tiamat (highest threat)

## Notes for DMs

1. **Prisoners are optional:** Can skip Plaza Sacrifice scene entirely if party doesn't stealth
2. **Foci order matters:** Destruction reduces hazards for Level 3
3. **Clock management:** Track it visually (printable tracker available in session-prep/)
4. **Tiamat manifestation:** Escalates challenge. Full Tiamat at Clock 8 is extremely deadly
5. **Rescued prisoners:** Can become allies but are weaker than party. Use them for emotional weight, not power scaling
6. **Post-campaign:** Stirling, Kess, Thorne, Marta, Fen can all become long-term allies for future campaigns
