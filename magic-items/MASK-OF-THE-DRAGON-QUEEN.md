# Mask of the Dragon Queen

**Wondrous Item, Artifact (requires attunement)**

---

## Description

Individually, the five dragon masks resemble the dragons they are named for. When two or more of the dragon masks are assembled, however, they transform magically into the Mask of the Dragon Queen. Each mask shrinks to become the modeled head of a chromatic dragon, appearing to roar its devotion to Tiamat where all the masks brought together are arranged crown-like on the wearer's head. Below the five masks, a new mask shapes itself, granting the wearer a draconic visage that covers the face, neck, and shoulders.

---

## Attunement

You must be attuned to this mask to gain its benefits. Attunement happens instantly upon first contact.

---

## Properties

### Base Features

**Damage Absorption (Passive)**
- You have resistance against acid, cold, fire, lightning, and poison damage.
- If you already have resistance to a damage type from another source, you instead have immunity.
- If you already have immunity from another source, whenever you take damage of that type, you take none of that damage and regain hit points equal to half the damage dealt.

**Draconic Majesty (Passive)**
- While you wear no armor, you can add your Charisma modifier to your AC.

**Dragon Sight (Active)**
- You gain darkvision out to 60 feet (or +60 feet if you already have darkvision).
- Once per day, you can gain blindsight out to 30 feet for 5 minutes.

**Dragon Tongue (Passive)**
- You can speak and understand Draconic.
- You have advantage on Charisma checks against dragons that share the color of the mask from which you chose your current properties.

**Legendary Resistance (5/Day)**
- If you fail a saving throw, you can choose to succeed instead.
- Recovers at dawn.

---

## Mask-Specific Abilities (Choose One)

While attuned, you can have **any of the properties from any one mask**. You can change which mask you're channeling as a bonus action.

### Red Dragon Mask: Dragon Fire
If you deal fire damage to a creature or flammable object, it starts burning. At the start of each of its turns, a creature burning in this way takes 1d6 fire damage. A creature that can reach the burning target can use an action to extinguish the fire.

### Blue Dragon Mask: Lingering Shock
If you deal lightning damage to a creature, it can't take reactions until its next turn.

### Black or Green Dragon Mask: Water Breathing
You can breathe underwater.

### White Dragon Mask: Winter's Fury
While your current hit points are equal to or less than half your hit point maximum, you deal an extra 1d8 cold damage with your melee attacks.

---

## Dragon Breath

If you have a breath weapon that requires rest to recharge, it gains a recharge of 6 (meaning it recharges on a 6 on a d6 at the start of your turn).

## DM Notes

**Attunement Speed:** Instant upon first touch.

**Mask Switching:** Takes a bonus action to switch active mask properties. The five-dragon crown is always visible when worn.

**Armor Interaction:** Draconic Majesty only applies if unarmored. If Frygtløs is in heavy armor, this property does not apply (but damage absorption always does).

**Legendary Resistance Recovery:** Refreshes at dawn. Once all 5 uses are spent, Frygtløs must finish a long rest to regain them.

**Resting:** Once attuned, the mask is magical armor—it does not need to be removed or "put down" to benefit from rests.

---

## Notable Interaction: Divine Word

Frygtløs is currently **blinded, deafened, and stunned** from Divine Word. The Mask does NOT cure these conditions—it is a magic item, not a spell or healing magic. These conditions persist until:
- The effect ends naturally (Divine Word stunned usually 1 turn)
- A *Greater Restoration* or *Dispel Magic* is cast
- Short or long rest (depends on the condition type)

**However:** The Mask's Damage Absorption provides significant survivability while he recovers.

---

## Artifact Status

This is a true **artifact** — it cannot be destroyed by normal means. If lost, it can be recovered through epic quests. Attunement cannot be forced; it requires willing contact.

  - He has 5 Legendary Resistances to use strategically
