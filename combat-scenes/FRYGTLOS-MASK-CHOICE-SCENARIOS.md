# Frygtløs' Choice — Round 13+ Scenarios

**Current State (end of Round 12):**
- Frygtløs: Stunned (can't act this round), Mask attuned, five drakessences clawing at his mind
- Tiamat: Aware he holds the Mask, recognizes his struggle, almost respectful
- Party: Critically wounded, waiting to see if he holds or breaks

---

## Scenario A: Rip the Mask Off (Rejection)

**If Frygtløs chooses to tear the crown from his head:**

> Hans hænder flytter op mod kronen. Ikke for at aktivere en kraft. For at *fjerne* den.
>
> Og fem stemmer skriger gennem hans hjerne samtidigt.
>
> Det røde hoved skригер *"MINE HOVEDER, MIN KRAFT, MIN VERDEN!"* Det blå brølер *"JEG VIL IKKE VÆKE ALENE IGEN!"* Alle fem samtidigt forsøger at låse hans arme på plads, at få ham til at *holde fast*, at få hans fingrene til at *give slip*.
>
> Hans musklatur ryster. Hans næse begynder at blø. Han *kan* få det af — hans vilje er stærk nok — men omkostningen er øjeblikkelig og brutal.

**Mechanical Cost:**

1. **Psychic Backlash:** 4d6 psychic damage (each drakessence fights removal)
   - No save; this is internal conflict damage
   - Triggers immediately as a reaction when he touches the Mask to remove it

2. **Strength Check DC 18** to physically pull it off
   - Success: Mask comes off, he's free but bloodied
   - Failure: Mask stays on, locked by drakewill, he takes the 4d6 and can't remove it this turn

3. **If Removed:** 
   - All dragon benefits vanish immediately
   - He's back to baseline Barbarian stats
   - Tiamat regains whatever power was being siphoned
   - Mask falls to ground; he can pick it up next turn (same struggle)

4. **If it Stays On:**
   - The five drakessences view his attempt as betrayal
   - Next turn, he gains 1 "Dragon Corruption" point (at 5, possession DC gets harder to resist)
   - Mask gets *tighter* — harder to remove next time (DC increases by 2)

**Narrative:**

> Hvis han får det af, så er der et øjeblik af ren *befrielse* — kulden af at være sig selv igen. Men hans øjne lukker sig. Hans næse bløder på alteret. Han mærker hver drakessens udgår gennem hans krop som hvis de blev revet ud ved rødderne. Han er free, men knust.
>
> Og Tiamat ruller tæt på ham, alle fem hoveder ned mod hans ansigt, og hun siger:
>
> *"Der er ingen drage i verden der gør det frivilligt. Engang du kastede det væk, kan du aldrig få den magt tilbage."*

**Combat Effect:**
- Round 13: He's back to baseline (no mask bonuses)
- Tiamat regains +1 AC, +1d6 damage per attack (her power returning)
- Party loses the Mask's damage absorption and Legendary Resistances
- He's weakened but whole

## Scenario B: Activate a Mask Property (Acceptance)

**If Frygtløs chooses to channel one of the five drakessences:**

He uses a bonus action to select a mask property (Red Dragon Fire, Blue Lingering Shock, etc.).

> Hans øjne åbner sig fuldt gennem masken. Han *accepterer* hvad han er blevet.
>
> Det koster ham alt.
>
> Først kommer en bølge af *varme* — ikke ild, men den drakessens særlige essens, der strømmer gennem hans hele krop. Hans hud blir tættere ved drakehud. Hans muskler waxer stærkere. Hans væbener der bliver skarpe som klør.
>
> Og samtidigt føler han en *sorg* der er så dyb at det næsten lukker hans hjerte helt ned. Hver kraft han kalder på, hver drakefarvning der fylder hans legeme, hver moment hvor han handler som draken handler — det er et slag mod hans sjæl. Hans sjæl *ved* at det er forkert, at det er imod alt han er, og det smerter som hvis verden selv ler ad ham.

**Mechanical Cost:**

1. **Activation Cost:** When he activates a mask property, he takes **2d8 psychic damage** (self-inflicted through psychic backlash of his own dragon-hatred)
   - This is automatic; no save
   - Happens immediately after choosing the property
   - Represents his own consciousness rebelling against what he's doing

2. **Every turn he uses that property:** +1d6 additional psychic damage per turn
   - If he switches mask properties next turn: new property, new damage (not cumulative)
   - Damage resets if he takes a full turn without activating anything

3. **Corruption Tracker:**
   - Each turn using a property: +1 Dragon Corruption
   - At 5 Corruption: Possession save becomes DC 16 (instead of DC 20) if five drakessences try to seize control
   - At 10 Corruption: They automatically seize control; he can't prevent it anymore

4. **But he gets full benefits:**
   - Damage Absorption (all five elements)
   - Legendary Resistance (5/day)
   - Chosen mask property (Dragon Fire, Lingering Shock, etc.)
   - +CHA to AC (Draconic Majesty)
   - Dragon Sight (sees through Divine Word blindness)

**Example: Round 13, Frygtløs Activates Red Dragon Fire**

> Frygtløs åbner sin hånd. Hans handflader glør rød.
>
> Han tager 2d8 psychic damage (let's say 11 damage).
>
> Han *skriger* — ikke fordi hans krop er skadet, men fordi hans *sind* er skadet. Han hader hvad han laver. Hver gang han bruger denne kraft, er det som hvis en del af ham dør en lille smule.
>
> Men hans næste slagsmål gør ekstra 1d6 fire damage. Målet der bliver ramt starter også at brænde for 1d6 per turn.
>
> Han er værktøjet for Tiamat's egen kraft. Og det *ødelægger* ham at erkende det.

**Corruption Consequence (at 5+):**
- Drakessenserne bliver boldigere
- Han skal lave saves for at bevare kontrol når han handler
- His actions might get "intercepted" — he intends to attack Tiamat, but the Red drakessence makes him attack a *party member* instead (contested action)
- By 10 Corruption: He's gone. The five dragons control his body fully.

**Combat Effect:**
- Round 13: He's a demigod warrior, but bleeding psychically
- He can action surge Barbarian + mask abilities
- Every turn costs him sanity
- Party gains powerful ally but watches him degrade in real-time
- Tiamat watches with grim satisfaction; she's *breaking* him

## Scenario C: Resist and Hold Control (Willpower)

**If Frygtløs chooses to keep the Mask but NOT activate any property this turn:**

He makes a **WIS saving throw DC 18** to maintain mental control over the five drakessences trying to possess him.

> Han står i stolen med kronet på hovedet. Han ryster. Hans tænder gnasker. Hans hele krop er en *knyttet næve* mod de fem draketanker der forsøger at åbne hans mund, at få hans øjne til at gløde, at få hans hænder til at løfte ham op og angribe hans venner.
>
> Han hører fem stemmer samtidigt. De taler i hans tonefald. De bruger hans minder. De *er* så tæt på at være ham.
>
> Og han siger: *"Nej."*

**Mechanical:**

1. **WIS Save DC 18** (each turn he holds without activating)
   - Success: He holds control. No damage, no corruption gained
   - Failure: He takes 3d6 psychic damage (drakessences clawing at him) + gains 1 Dragon Corruption

2. **No mask benefits this turn:**
   - He's not channeling any property
   - Damage Absorption still active (passive)
   - Legendary Resistances available (can still use them)
   - But no active powers, no damage boost

3. **Legendary Resistance usage:**
   - He can use his 5 Legendary Resistances to help party members if needed
   - *"You fail that save? No, you don't."* — he channels one LR to save an ally
   - This costs him nothing extra; it's passive artifact function

4. **Tiamat's Response:**
   - She sees him *resisting* the corruption
   - She respects the stubborn refusal
   - But she also knows it's temporary; eventually the five will break through

**Narrative:**

> Frygtløs holder stille. Ikke bevægelse. Kun *vilje*.
>
> Varmen omkring hans hovede bliver til *tryk*. Som hvis hele templet selv presser ned på hans pande. Hans blod ulmer. Hans musklatur føler som hvis den skal sprænge ud gennem hans hud.
>
> Men han holder.
>
> Ikke for ham. For partiet. For verden der venter uden for.
>
> Tiamat mærker hans modstand. Og for første gang siden han tog kronet på, ser hun *ærbødighed* i hans kamp. Hun respekterer manden der lider for ikke at blive monster.
>
> Hun respekterer det længe nok til at sende næste slag mod resten af partiet i stedet.

**Combat Effect:**
- Round 13: He's stalemate with the five drakessences
- Party gets breathing room (he's not aggressive, but he's not helping with damage either)
- He can support with Legendary Resistance (let allies reroll saves)
- Long-term unsustainable (can't hold forever; eventually possession takes hold)
- But buys time for party to finish Tiamat before corruption reaches 10

## Which Path Is Most Frygtløs?

**A (Rip It Off):**
- Pure defiance, pure rejection of compromise
- Immediate relief, longer-term weakness
- *"I will not be a dragon"* — pride over pragmatism

**B (Channel Powers):**
- Pragmatic sacrifice, self-destruction for victory
- Becomes increasingly unstable
- *"I will become the monster to save them"* — desperation over dignity
- Likely ends in possession/corruption unless he can finish Tiamat before Corruption reaches 10

**C (Resist and Hold):**
- Middle path: acceptance without activation
- Sustainable for a few rounds, then crumbles
- *"I will carry this burden without letting it carry me"* — discipline over desperation
- Best for supporting party tactically
